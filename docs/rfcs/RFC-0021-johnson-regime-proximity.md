## Summary

The proximity test of the advice and weight commitments draws its free combination coefficients as independent uniform `Fp2` values squeezed right after the seed, instead of the tensor `eq(ξ*, row)`. Advice moves from after the stages to the seed; unmasked weight points already follow the seed under RFC-0049, while their tensor generator changes to the free-row stream. Proximity soundness is accounted up to the Johnson radius with two conjecture-free bounds whose error is linear in the code length: the correlated-agreement theorem of Ben-Sasson, Carmon, Haböck, Kopparty and Saraf (2025) and the mutual-correlated-agreement bound of Dao, Kominers and Thaler (2026) as formalized in Lean in ArkLib. Each column query then yields about 0.49 bits at rate 1/2 instead of 0.415 and 0.94 bits at rate 1/4 instead of 0.66. New versions of the parameter sets open 130 advice columns instead of 155 and 68 weight columns instead of 97 in live mode (163 and 85 instead of 193 and 122 non-interactively), which removes about 0.12 GB from the 2 GB proof and 16% of the verifier's column hashing and inner products, without an extra tensor-regeneration pass, but with the O(M) transcript-squeeze work below. Its earlier adversarial readings also found three defects, already repaired upstream by pull requests #244 and #245: the blinding claims of RFC-0013 and RFC-0012 are absorbed before the batching coefficients and each carries its own coefficient, so that the evaluation checks bind every claim (issue #237), and every `Fp2` combined row is masked by two `Fp` blinding rows instead of one, so that it is uniform as RFC-0013 intends. Every number in this RFC is a projection (08 §1) computed from the cited statements (E0).

Fresh review on 2026-10-19 supports scoped theorem/default-number checks, not full protocol approval ([review3](../../research/experiments/EXP-0005-list-decoding-extractor/review3.md)). Joint mask use remains an acceptance blocker (OQ-0021-7); protocol/schema and live-target decisions remain open. The numerical weight profiles below retain masked P_W=65536 as a conservative planning envelope; protocol/1 operational weights have P=0 under RFC-0049 and need protocol-specific calculator output. Historical results are not relabelled as exact unmasked values.

## Motivation

RFC-0014 Alternatives Considered 3 rejected list-decoding analysis because "provable bounds in that regime carry field-size terms that erode the margin, and conjectured bounds are not acceptable for treaty use". The first clause was true of the bound then available ([BCIKS20], whose Johnson-regime error is `(k+1)² / ((2 · min(η, √ρ/20))^7 · q)`: at most 57 bits at Tessera's sizes, EXP-0001) and is no longer true: [BCHKS25] Theorem 1.5 and [DKT26] replace it by terms linear in `n`, which keep 82 to 86 bits at the chosen parameters. The second clause stands and this RFC needs no conjecture; the capacity regime, which would halve the columns again, is closed for Tessera's codes by the 2025 and 2026 negative results ([CS25], [KKH26], [Kam26]).

The current tensor generator `eq(ξ*, row)` is covered in the Johnson regime only by [BCGM26] Theorem 5 at about `2^-54`, so the proposed query reduction needs independent coefficients. Main already carries the tensor factor in `ϑ·n/|Fp2|`: `ϑ=⌈log2 R⌉` for allocated advice rows, and `ϑ_T=⌈log2 m_T⌉` for unmasked weights indexed by committed positions (RFC-0049) (issue #254), binds the blinding claims before batching (issue #237), and uses two base-field rows per extension-field mask (issue #239). This Draft preserves those repairs. Its new work is the early uniform coefficient stream and the Johnson-regime bound, rather than reapplying the historical fixes.

## Goals

- Opened columns `τ_A = 130` and `τ_W = 68` at 64 one-shot bits (live), `τ_A = 163` and `τ_W = 85` at 80 one-shot bits plus 20 grinding bits (non-interactive), each with a field-size term of at least 82 one-shot bits.
- A soundness argument for the opening protocol in the list-decoding regime that rests only on cited, machine-checked statements, carried in this RFC and read adversarially before acceptance.
- The list-binding argument carried to the merged evaluation checks, whose blinding claims are bound (pull request #244), and to the `Fp2` blinding rows (pull request #245).
- Both soundness calculators (`tessera params check`, `tessera-verify params`) evaluate the new row exactly and agree on a pinned vector.

## Non-Goals

- Changing the code, the rate or the mask sizes (rate 1/4 for the advice is refuted as a default by EXP-0004; folded or multiplicity codes would reach capacity at the price of a new encoder, RFC-0011 Alternatives Considered 2 and 3).
- Smaller combined rows (a Johnson-regime IOPP for the `G + 1` resident combined rows is a separate idea, ledger queue).
- Any change to the committed-model domain/trust distinction of RFC-0048: uniqueness and fidelity have separate premises.
- Any change to the sumcheck, LogUp or tree rows of the budget. RFC-0013's masks, blinding rows included, are over `Fp2` since pull request #245 (OQ-0021-5); this RFC takes them as specified.

## Proposed Design

### Conventions

One rate convention serves every formula of this RFC: `D = k + P − 1` is the maximum degree of a codeword (the code's dimension is `D + 1 = k'`), `ρ₋ = D/n`, and the agreement threshold at slack `η` is `A_thr = ⌈(√ρ₋ + η) · n⌉`, computed by integer comparison (`A_thr` is the least integer `A ≥ ηn` with `(A − ηn)² ≥ D · n`), never with floating point. The proximity radius is `γ = 1 − √ρ₋ − η` and the bits per query `b_J = −log2(√ρ₋ + η)`. RFC-0014's `ρ' = k'/n` differs from `ρ₋` by `1/n`; the two give the same bits per query to five decimals but can differ by one in `A_thr`, which a pinned vector must not. Field elements of `Fp2` are written `a + b·i` over the basis `{1, i}` of RFC-0002.

### Proximity coefficients

RFC-0011 'Opening protocol' step 3 becomes:

3. **Proximity coefficients.** One independent uniform `Fp2` element per free physical non-zero row (excluding the fixed row or pair defined below), squeezed from the transcript immediately after the seed (RFC-0014 'Transcript schedule', the new order 3b below) by successive `challenge_fp2` calls under the label `pcs/advice/xi`: the i-th call is the coefficient of the i-th free physical non-zero row in the specified order, with RFC-0004's transcript-global counter and ratchet (RFC-0004 'Transcript', the `squeeze` operation). Both sides derive the stream from the transcript; it is never sent. The weight commitments draw theirs under `pcs/weights/{T}/xi` in the same way and at the same place.

Steps 4 to 8 are unchanged: the prover accumulates `u_ξ = Σ_row ξ_row · A_row` and `M_ξ` in the opening pass, the verifier checks `Σ_row ξ_row · col_j[row] = enc(u_ξ)(x_j) + Z_H(x_j) · M_ξ(x_j)` at every queried column. One row of the combination carries the fixed coefficient 1, so that the combination is exactly the affine-space generator of the cited statements (one fixed coefficient, the rest uniform): from v0.4 it is the proximity row's `Fp2` blinding row `Q_ξ = Q_ξ^{(0)} + X·Q_ξ^{(1)}` of RFC-0013 'Hiding openings' (two committed `Fp` rows entering with the fixed coefficients `1` and `X`, together the one fixed word of the affine space), for advice, placed after the claim groups’ blinding pairs in the final segment, `S − 1`, after its epilogue and table tiles. Ordinary tile rows, alignment gaps, and segment boundaries follow RFC-0046; the global stream traverses segments in index order and rows within each segment in layout order, skipping public zero rows. The appended blinding rows count in the advice descriptor’s `rows` and hence in `commitment_id` (RFC-0031, INV-PCS-1); in v0.3, which has no blinding rows, it is the first non-zero row in layout order, which then draws no coefficient. A weight matrix has no segments: traverse its non-zero data rows and then its epoch blinding rows in physical order (RFC-0012/RFC-0040). In the six rows publicly reserved before Round1, the first pair is the evaluation mask, the second pair is the fixed proximity word, and the third pair is the extension mask; a linking opening’s four reserved rows use the first two roles. These roles and indices are fixed by the public reservation before the seed, never selected from stage values. Skip exactly the designated proximity pair when drawing free coefficients.

Let M be the number of physical non-zero rows. There are C=M−1 free coefficients in an unmasked opening, and C=M−2 in a masked opening; fixed rows make no challenge call. Transcript vectors must pin this exact count, including both components of the masked pair. The cost is O(M) squeezes per opening (about 5.8×10^5 at 16K tokens: 18.6 MB of hash output for 9.3 MB of coefficients), which both sides already match when they evaluate `c_g` for every group.

These formulas apply when the designated fixed row or pair exists. For unmasked M=0, retain RFC-0049: no proximity row, no xi calls, no queries row or column-answer component for that matrix. Do not serialize a zero placeholder proximity row. Its public commitment still has the canonical d_j[0] leaves. Any logical claim on the publicly zero tensor is derived and checked as zero; an absent opening does not create a new batching challenge or evaluation entry. Preserve every other draw/absorb required by the active-matrix schedule. OQ-0021-6 must pin those matrix-id sets and their canonical messages. A masked matrix with reserved blinding rows has M>0 even when it has no live data row, so it is not this branch.

`M` is distinct from the allocated row span `R` of [RFC-0047](RFC-0047-advice-order.md#ν). The current advice tensor generator's factor is `ϑ = ⌈log2 R⌉`, with `ν = log2 k + ⌈log2 R⌉`, including allocated padding. This proposal preserves RFC-0047's tensor layout order, permutation of claim coordinates into that order, maximum layout and sequence-length rules. Skipping public zero rows in the free-coefficient stream does not shrink the allocated tensor domain or redefine its proximity point.

The final public R, descriptor row count and maximum-layout check include every appended advice blinding pair. Slots and fixed-pair indices are reserved before the seed from the public schedule; stage-value-dependent grouping cannot choose them retroactively. The joint hiding argument for all these rows is an acceptance gate (OQ-0021-7), not a consequence of assigning them role names.

Why right after the seed: the exceptional set of the mutual-correlated-agreement statement depends only on the committed matrix and the radius, both fixed by Round 1, so an early `ξ` costs the argument nothing; and a challenge squeezed after the sumcheck stages can be re-rolled in non-interactive mode through the stages' final evaluations, which have slack (several values constrained by one check), without redoing the seed's proof of work. Squeezed right after the seed, a retry of `ξ` costs the 20 bits of `seed-pow`, as RFC-0014 'Grinding' intends.

### Binding the blinding claims

RFC-0013 'Hiding openings' and RFC-0012 step 3 bind the blinding claims since pull request #244 (issue #237, found by the first adversarial reading of this RFC's argument): each group's `q_g = ⟨Q_g, eq(col_g)⟩` and each weight matrix's `q_T` are absorbed under `pcs/advice/blinding` and `pcs/weights/{T}/blinding` before `γ` and `θ_T` are squeezed (RFC-0014 row 7); the group check reads `Σ_t γ^{t+1}·v_t + q_g = ⟨u'_g, eq(col_g)⟩` with no claim at the coefficient 1, and the weight check `θ_T·v_T + q_T = ⟨u_T, eq(r_o^lo) ⊗ eq(r_i)⟩`. A false claim then passes with probability at most `T_g/|Fp2|` per group of `T_g` claims and `1/|Fp2|` per matrix. Before #244, `q_g` was absorbed with the combined rows after the challenges and entered the check with coefficient 1, so a prover could solve for it, and RFC-0012's check `v_T + q_T` had no challenge at all; the earlier revisions of this RFC carried the repair as new orders 7a and 7b with one independent coefficient per claim. This revision adopts the merged shape, whose only effect on the argument is the factor `T_g` in the claim-binding term (step 3 below), and drops its own.

The hiding defect the second reading found, one `Fp` blinding row for an `Fp2` combined row, is issue #239, fixed by pull request #245: every mask of RFC-0013 is uniform over `Fp2`, an `Fp2` row being two committed `Fp` rows `Q = Q^{(0)} + X·Q^{(1)}` (`Fp2 = Fp[X]/(X^2 − 7)`, RFC-0002) that enter a combination with the coefficients `1` and `X`; the advice has 34 blinding rows per proof, a weight matrix six per proof (two each for the evaluation row, the proximity row and the mask row), and `B_W = 4096` keeps about 681 proofs per epoch (RFC-0012 'Budget state machine'). With `c_g(row) = a_row + b_row·X`, one `Fp` row masked only an `Fp`-subspace of dimension `k` of `Fp2^k` and left `Σ_row b_row·A_row` in the clear, about 2^20 `Fp` combinations per weight matrix per proof. What this RFC needs of the merged design:

- **The fixed word.** The proximity row's blinding row `Q_ξ` enters with coefficient 1, its rows with `1` and `X`, and carries no claim (RFC-0013 'The proximity row'); it is the fixed word `w_0` of the affine space `w_0 + Σ_row ξ_row · A_row` of the cited statements, and its two rows draw no `ξ` coefficient. Every other non-zero row, the groups' blinding rows included, draws one.
- **Order.** The blinding claims are absorbed at row 7 before `γ` and `θ_T`, as #244 specifies; the stage claims `v_t` and `v_T` are absorbed earlier (orders 5 and 6).
- **Binding.** With the claims and the blinding claims fixed before the challenges, a wrong claim in a group makes the check a non-zero polynomial of degree at most `T_g` in `γ`, zero with probability at most `T_g/|Fp2|` (the argument's step 3); a wrong weight claim makes it a non-zero affine function of `θ_T`, zero with probability `1/|Fp2|`.

### Soundness argument (list binding)

RFC-0011's soundness paragraph decodes every row uniquely. Beyond the unique-decoding radius the committed matrix may be close to several codeword matrices, and the argument becomes list binding plus per-proof consistency. The argument of [EXP-0005](../../research/experiments/EXP-0005-list-decoding-extractor/README.md), with the corrections of its two readings, is the security section of this RFC; its steps, each on a cited statement:

0. **The committed matrix.** `U` is the matrix of non-zero rows (blinding rows included) whose symbols open the Merkle leaves through the chained segment digests of RFC-0011 'Column digests and root'; two different column vectors opening the same leaf are a collision (the budget's hash row). Zero rows are public and skipped; the coefficient stream is indexed over the non-zero rows.
   - In masked mode apply the affine theorem to the virtual matrix whose fixed word is `U_Q0 + X·U_Q1` and whose other rows are the remaining physical rows over `Fp2`. Its C=M−2 free coefficients are independent and uniform; the pair is one fixed word, not two independent coefficient draws. The map `(U_Q0,U_Q1)↦U_Q0+X·U_Q1` preserves the common agreement set because the physical entries and evaluation points lie in `Fp`. After extraction, split the fixed polynomial into its `Fp` real/imaginary coefficient polynomials to recover both physical rows. This supplies the bridge from the cited affine theorem to the physical matrix; the other physical rows use step4 below. The public empty-matrix branch is checked directly as specified above.
1. **The proximity check pins one matrix.** With the coefficients above, the `ξ` column check either fails with probability at least `1 − (1 − γ)^τ`, or the sent combined row is a codeword within distance `γ` of `ξ · U` (its shape, `k` values plus `P` mask coefficients, bounds its degree below `k'`; RFC-0011 step 6, `Reject(Evaluation)`). Outside an exceptional set of coefficient vectors of density at most `ε_MCA = E₀/(|Fp2| − 1)`, fixed by `U` and `γ` before `ξ` is drawn, that codeword is `ξ · Q*` for one codeword matrix `Q*` whose rows agree with the committed rows on exactly the set `A` of columns where the check passes ([ArkLib26] `exists_affine_exceptionalSet_full_agreement_of_exactLine`, `LineToAffine.lean` lines 193 to 207, whose conclusion is an equality of agreement sets and whose exceptional set depends on `U` and the radius only, with the `LineExactAgreementBound` built in the proof of `mcaError_affineLine_johnson_le`, `Johnson/Probability.lean` lines 50 to 62, from `exists_johnson_line_exactCorrelatedPair_ceil`, `Johnson/Agreement.lean` lines 104 to 117, and `E₀ = johnsonExceptionCount`; hypotheses `1 ≤ D ≤ n − 2`, `η > 0`, `√ρ₋ + η ≤ 1`, `A_thr ≤ ⌈n(1 − γ)⌉` and `k' ≤ n(1 − γ)`, the last by a margin of at least 1.068×10^6 points at every parameter set; no characteristic hypothesis; the same bound in every dimension).
2. **Every group is bound to that matrix.** For every claim group `g`, the sent combined row equals `c_g · Q*` as a polynomial, or every query that passes both checks lies in a set of at most `D` columns, so the prover survives with probability at most `(D/n)^τ`. The group coefficients need no property of their own, and the bound covers every group at once.
3. **The claims bind to its messages.** With the blinding claims absorbed before the challenges and the claims of a group at the coefficients `γ^{t+1}` (RFC-0011 step 2, RFC-0013 'Hiding openings'), a wrong claim in a group makes the group check a non-zero polynomial of degree at most `T_g` in `γ`, zero with probability at most `T_g/|Fp2|`; the prover's choice of `Q*` among the codeword matrices close to `U` costs a union over their number `L`, so the term is `Σ_g T_g · L/|Fp2| ≤ G · T_max · L/|Fp2|`, with `L ≤ ⌊n(A_thr − D)/(A_thr² − nD)⌋`, the machine-checked pairwise Johnson list bound for words ([ArkLib26] `closePolynomialSet_finite_and_ncard_le_pairwiseJohnson`, `ReedSolomon/ListDecodability/PairwiseJohnson.lean` lines 75 to 81 and 164 to 168, hypotheses `D + 1 ≤ A_thr` and `nD < A_thr²`), carried to matrices by the separating-functional reduction of EXP-0005 'The list size': 49 at `η = 0.003` for the advice and at `η = 0.005` for the weights. A weight matrix's check is affine in `θ_T` and contributes `L/|Fp2|`.
4. **The physical rows are over `Fp`.** An `Fp2` polynomial of degree below `k'` that takes `Fp` values on at least `k'` points of `D ⊆ Fp` is fixed by the Frobenius automorphism. This applies to every extracted physical row other than the fixed virtual word. That word is split as in step0; its two component polynomials have degree below `k'` and match `U_Q0` and `U_Q1` on the same agreement set. The recovered physical messages are therefore `Fp` values; the virtual fixed polynomial itself need not be Frobenius-fixed.
5. **What is one-shot and what is re-rollable.** `ξ` is one-shot per session in live mode (one seed per `Round1`, INV-PROTO-1 and INV-PROTO-2) and costs `seed-pow` per retry in non-interactive mode (it depends on the state right after the seed). The queries are the verifier's in live mode (INV-PROTO-3) and cost `query-pow` per retry in non-interactive mode. The batching challenges `γ` and `θ_T` and the stage challenges follow prover messages with slack and are re-rollable in both modes at one or more hashes per attempt, so their terms are budgeted as `λ = −log2(term)` in RFC-0014's `2^{t−λ}` sense, never as one-shot: the claim-binding term is `2^-110.4` at 256 claims per group, the worst case EXP-0005 tabulates, and `2^-113.1` at RFC-0011's target of at most 40.

The statement of uniqueness changes accordingly. RFC-0011's "each row decodes uniquely and the evaluation checks bind every claim to the decoded rows" becomes: **outside the charged exceptional, binding and query-failure events, an accepted proof is consistent with exactly one decoded matrix `Q*`, and every claim, blinding claims included, is an evaluation of that matrix's messages.** List binding is per proof: two accepted proofs of one epoch consistent with different weight matrices need agreement sets with `|A_1| + |A_2| ≤ n + D`, so the pair is accepted with probability at most `((1 + ρ₋)/2)^{2τ_W} = 2^-89.8`, and a matrix that makes either acceptable with non-negligible probability makes the other fail at `(D/n)^τ`. This is a conditional single-pair query calculation, excluding field/hash/batching events and any epoch-wide union or retry accounting. SP-3 ([06 §4](../spec/06-security.md#4-security-properties)) is unaffected: it speaks of the witness of a valid statement, and Lemma 0 of RFC-0009 and the range analysis of RFC-0006 apply to `Q*`'s messages as they applied to the uniquely decoded rows.

### Soundness budget

The proximity row of RFC-0014 'Soundness budget' becomes

| Component | Error bound | Live mode | Non-interactive mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proximity per commitment | `(1 − γ)^τ + E₀/(\|Fp2\| − 1) + Σ_g T_g · L/\|Fp2\| + (D/n)^τ`, `γ = 1 − √ρ₋ − η` | `τ · b_J ≥ 64` bits and `log2(\|Fp2\|/E₀) ≥ 64` bits, one shot per session; the two small terms `λ ≥ 100` | `τ · b_J + g_query ≥ 100`; `log2(\|Fp2\|/E₀) + g_seed ≥ 100`; the two small terms `λ ≥ 100` without grinding credit |

with `E₀ = johnsonExceptionCount(n, D, A_thr, η)` evaluated exactly in rational arithmetic from the definitions of [ArkLib26] `FiniteBounds.lean` lines 66 to 108 (`m = max(⌈√ρ₋/(2η)⌉, 3)`, `t = m + 1/2`, `μ = ⌈t/√ρ₋⌉ − 1`, `h = ⌈t²/(3ρ₋)⌉ − 1`, `θ = (n − D)/(A_thr − D)`, `E₀ = (2μ − 1)h + θ(h + μ + 4Dμh) + (n − D − 1)μ`, every ceiling of a square root by integer comparison), `L` from the pairwise formula above, `G` from the claim list. The targets are per commitment, as in RFC-0014; a proof has one advice commitment and ten weight matrices (RFC-0012), a union of about 3.5 bits that the calculators print; whether SP-1's "per live audit" means per proof is OQ-0021-4. The two small terms are at or below `2^-110` at every parameter set below (E0, [EXP-0005 results](../../research/experiments/EXP-0005-list-decoding-extractor/results/terms.txt)). The row replaces main’s tensor-generator field term `ϑ·n/|Fp2|` (issue #254); the historical `n/|Fp2|` text was already corrected upstream.

RFC-0014 'Grinding' is restated: the seed's proof of work adds 20 bits to a bound only when the challenge it concerns is squeezed before any prover message with slack; `ξ` qualifies after this RFC, the batching coefficients and the stage challenges do not and are budgeted as `λ` without credit.

### Parameter sets

New versions; the identifiers `live-64/1` and `ni-100/1` keep their meaning for proofs that name them.

Numbers from `python3 -I research/tools/proximity.py --bchks25 --arklib` and `research/experiments/EXP-0005-list-decoding-extractor/terms.py` (E0, projections). `η_A = 0.003` dominates 0.002 for the advice: the same 130 live columns with 82.2 field bits and a list of 49 instead of 80.4 bits and 73; non-interactively 162 columns would meet 80 query bits by 0.01 bit, so `τ_A = 163` buys half a bit of margin for one column. Margins over the targets the calculators enforce: live advice 18.2 bits on the field term and 0.2 on the query bits; non-interactive advice 2.2 and 0.5; weights 22.0 and 0.1 (live), 6.0 and 0.1 (non-interactive). Smaller `η` buys one more column at the cost of 3 field bits per halving (`τ_A = 129` at `η = 0.001` with 77.4 bits). The Johnson radius caps the gain: bits per query tend to `−log2 √ρ₋ = 0.5` at rate 1/2, so `τ_A ≥ 128` at 64 bits whatever the bound; this RFC takes 130 of a possible 128.

Sizes at the default parameters (RFC-0011 'Sizes'): opened columns `130 × 5.8×10^5 × 8 B ≈ 0.60 GB` (was 0.72 GB), non-interactive `0.76 GB` (was 0.90 GB); combined rows unchanged in count and size (the two blinding rows per combination are committed rows, not sent rows); the proof falls from about 2.0 to about 1.88 GB; the verifier hashes 0.60 GB of columns and computes `(G + 1) · 130` inner products of length 5.8×10^5 (was 155).

### Soundness calculator

`tessera params check` and `tessera-verify params` (independent code) evaluate `A_thr`, `E₀` and `L` in exact integer and rational arithmetic from the definitions above, print `γ`, `b_J`, the query bits, `log2(|Fp2|/E₀)`, `L`, the two small terms as `λ`, the per-proof union, and which proof of work covers each row (as the comment on #103 asks), and fail when any bound misses its target. The conformance vector of the four parameter sets (`A_thr`, query bits, field bits, `L`, `E₀` as a rational) is pinned under `spec/vectors/`; a change to the Lean definitions that moves a value is a specification change.

### Invariants

- **INV-PCS-5.** The proximity coefficients of a commitment are independent uniform `Fp2` elements squeezed immediately after the seed, with coefficient 1 on the row the layout designates; a tensor, power or otherwise structured generator is not used for the proximity row, and no prover message is absorbed between the seed and the coefficients.
- **INV-PCS-6.** Every blinding claim is absorbed before the coefficient that multiplies it is squeezed, and claim t of a group carries γ^(t+1), with no claim at coefficient 1; a `Round2Response` whose blinding claims are missing or misordered fails the shape check with `Reject(Evaluation)`, and one whose claims are reordered fails the group check itself.
- **INV-PCS-7.** The proximity row's slack `η` and the resulting `τ` come from the named parameter set; the calculators reject a set whose field term or query bits miss the target.
- **INV-PCS-8 (unresolved; OQ-0021-7).** Every revealed `Fp2` combination must have sufficient conditionally fresh masking for the joint malicious-verifier view. The current stream also gives free xi coefficients to other groups' blinding rows and other epoch blinders; a designated pair can therefore appear in another revealed combination. This conflicts with the literal one-use statement of INV-ZK-2. Role reservation alone does not resolve it: acceptance requires a joint simulator/rank argument and an explicit compatible invariant, or a revised construction and budget. No leak is asserted solely from this syntactic conflict.

## Alternatives Considered

1. **Keep the `eq(ξ*, row)` generator and account it in the Johnson regime.** Rejected: the only Johnson-regime statement that covers polynomial generators ([BCGM26] Theorem 5) carries an `n²` term and lands near `2^-54` at `n = 2^23` ([SUR-0001](../../research/surveys/SUR-0001-proximity.md)); the current tensor generator uses `⌈log2 R⌉` coordinates for advice and `⌈log2 m_T⌉` for unmasked weights, versus O(M) free-row squeezes here (under20 MB of hash output in the reference advice profile).
2. **[BCIKS20]'s Johnson-regime bound.** Refuted by [EXP-0001](../../research/experiments/EXP-0001-list-decoding-tau/README.md): the error term caps at 57 bits for every slack, so `τ_A` could fall only to 149.
3. **Capacity-regime (conjectured) bounds, `τ_A ≈ 64`.** Rejected, as in RFC-0014: conjectures are inadmissible, and they are false for RS codes over smooth multiplicative cosets of prime fields in a window below capacity ([CS25], [KKH26], [Kam26]).
4. **Rate 1/4 for the advice (`τ_A` 95 under unique decoding, 65 under this RFC).** Refuted as a default by [EXP-0004](../../research/experiments/EXP-0004-advice-rate-quarter/README.md): `μ` rises 10 to 21% for 14 to 21% less proof. Available later as a size-oriented parameter set.
5. **Other slacks.** `η_A = 0.002` gives the same live columns with 80.4 field bits and `L = 73`, a 0.4-bit margin over the non-interactive target; `η_A = 0.02` keeps 90 bits at `τ_A = 140`. The chosen 0.003 keeps 82.2 bits at 130 and is exact, not asymptotic.
6. **Keep `ξ` at transcript order 7 and add a proof of work (`xi-pow`) before it.** Considered as the alternative repair of the non-interactive retry: it costs the honest prover 2^20 hashes and leaves the stages' slack as a retry surface for every later challenge. For advice and a proposed masked-weight schedule, squeezing xi after the seed requires no additional proof of work and removes this late retry surface; unmasked weights already use that position under RFC-0049. Uniform free-row draws still cost O(M) hashes; the batching coefficients stay where they are and are budgeted as `λ`. The historical blinding-claim ordering repair already landed in #244. This proposal preserves it; combined rows still follow the stages.
7. **Bind the blinding claims by committing to them in Round 1.** Considered: a hash of every `q_g` in `Round1` would also fix them before the coefficients. Rejected: the claims depend on the stage challenges, which are not known at Round 1; absorbing them before the challenges at row 7, as #244 does, is the minimal repair.
8. **One `Fp` blinding row with an `Fp2` coefficient.** Rejected: whatever the coefficient, one `Fp` row spans an `Fp`-subspace of dimension `k` and leaves the complementary `k` coordinates of the `Fp2^k` combination in the clear; two rows as one virtual `Fp2` row are the minimal exact mask, and two rows with independent `Fp2` coefficients would leave a `2^-64` dependence event instead. Pull request #245 merged the two-row design.

## Drawbacks

- The commitment binds to a list and the protocol to one consistent list element per proof, a subtler statement than unique decoding; the argument has had two independent adversarial readings and the maintainer's own review remains.
- The budget depends on definitions in an external, evolving Lean library; the RFC pins them by transcription (above) and by commit (`Verified-zkEVM/ArkLib` at `4dae3acff846125521b7b3998010885071746f16`), and the calculators carry the transcription, not a dependency.
- The ePrint and STOC versions of [BCHKS25] and the paper text of [DKT26] were not read (ePrint served PDFs behind a challenge on 2026-10-16); a constant that differs there changes the table by at most a few bits against margins of 18 bits (live) and 2.2 bits (non-interactive) on the field term.
- The transcript derives O(M) coefficients instead of O(log R) for advice or O(log m_T) for unmasked weights. It moves the existing xi labels; the blinding fields and two-row masks already belong to the accepted zero-knowledge schedule. The proposed budget is unchanged, but OQ-0021-7 must establish that it suffices before masked adoption.

## Migration / Rollout

RFC-0036 already reserves `tessera-protocol/1` for the unmasked schema and `/2` for zero knowledge. Neither identifier may acquire a different xi schedule. Before acceptance, assign fresh protocol identifiers for the unmasked and masked schedules, specify their schema mapping and bind it in `SessionContext` (OQ-0021-6). The proposal must also supersede RFC-0036’s row-7 schedule: keep the gamma labels there and move only xi to row 3b, leaving RFC-0032’s identity and tree masks and RFC-0037/RFC-0045’s split absorptions in place.

v0.3 delivers the coefficient stream at order 3b, the new budget row in both calculators with the pinned vector, and the parameter sets `live-64/2` and `ni-100/2`; `live-64/1` and `ni-100/1` remain accepted by the release verifier until `tessera-protocol/1` freezes, after which only the version-2 sets are current. The blinding-claim binding (issue #237, pull request #244) and the `Fp2` masks (issue #239, pull request #245) are in RFC-0012, RFC-0013 and RFC-0014 already; v0.4 implements them with the zero-knowledge messages.

## Testing Strategy

- Calculators: both implementations reproduce the pinned vector (`A_thr`, query bits, field bits, `L`, `E₀`) for the four sets and agree on 1,000 random size profiles; a profile of `ni-100/2` with `η = 0.0005` makes the field term miss 80 bits (74.4) and the check fail, proving it is live.
- Proximity (unique-decoding regression): the RFC-0011 test with one row at relative distance `δ ∈ {0.05, 0.25, 0.5}` is rejected at least at rate `1 − (1 − δ)^τ`.
- Soundness calibration over `F65537` (07, 'Tests that count' 4): a committed matrix within distance `γ` of two codeword matrices, with a cheating prover that answers one group from each, is rejected at the rate step 2 of the argument predicts, `(|A ∩ B_g|/n)^τ ≤ (D/n)^τ`.
- Adversarial, blinding claims: a prover that answers a group with a wrong `v_t` and a compensating `q_g`, and a weight opening with `(v_T + Δ, q_T − Δ)`, are rejected with `Reject(Evaluation)`; the test is seen red against the RFC-0013 order before the fix.
- Hiding: the distinguisher suite of 07 §7 gains a test on `Im(u'_g)` and on `(b · Re − a · Im)(u'_g)` for two witnesses with a coefficient `a + b·i`; it is red against one blinding row and green against two.
- Adversarial, retry surface: in non-interactive mode, changing a stage's final evaluations within the stage's check changes the batching coefficients but not `ξ`; a proof whose `ξ` stream is derived from any later transcript state fails `Reject(Proximity)`.
- Coefficient stream: transcript vectors for both modes include the exact free-coefficient count, the first and last proximity coefficients and the first batching coefficient of each commitment; prover and verifier derive identical streams for the conformance models.
- Empty weights: an unmasked no-ChannelParams model has no proximity/query component and makes zero xi calls for that matrix, preserving RFC-0049. Pin active matrix-id sets and the next commitment's first challenge to catch a spurious draw or zero placeholder; distinguish masked reserved rows.
- Adversarial: an opening that uses a tensor or power generator for the proximity row is rejected (the verifier derives the stream itself and the column check fails with `Reject(Proximity)`).
- Reproduction of the numbers: `python3 -I research/tools/proximity.py --bchks25 --arklib` and `python3 -I research/experiments/EXP-0005-list-decoding-extractor/terms.py`.
