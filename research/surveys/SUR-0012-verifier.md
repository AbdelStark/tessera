## Synthesis

**1. The verification budget is a NTT budget, and SC-3 is not where the gap is.** Pricing RFC-0011's verifier-work row with this repository's unit costs ([E0-SUR12] over [EXP-0002](../experiments/EXP-0002-commit-cost-calibration/README.md), [EXP-0007](../experiments/EXP-0007-pruned-column-answer-pass/README.md) and today's [E1-SUR12]) gives about 13 s on one core: 7.5 s for the 17 coset NTTs of size 2^23 plus the 17 interpolation NTTs of size 2^22 over `Fp2` that `enc(u_g)` needs and the row omits (the step IDEA-0002 deletes), 2.2 s of inner products, about 2 s of decoding, 0.7 s of table MLEs, 0.6 s of column hashing. RFC-0019's "about 15 s" matches, but its "32 cores" is a misattribution: the verifier as written has no thread (`rayon` is allowlisted in `verifier/allowlist.toml` and used by no crate), and every one of the five terms is embarrassingly parallel over columns, rows or tables, so 32 cores would bring the figure to about 0.4 s. The scoreboard's "about 500×" against Attestable compares the SC-3 budget (300 s) with a vendor's measurement (157–648 ms); the projection-to-claim gap is 20–80× on one core and 1–3× on 32. What a 2 GB proof costs that a 5 MiB proof does not is transfer and decoding: 17 s at 1 Gbit/s, which no CPU parallelism touches. The structural gap is therefore `size`, as [baseline.md §5](../baseline.md#5-scoreboard) already says, and the honest verify metric for a non-succinct proof is "transfer plus check", which SC-3 does not state. This confirms RFC-0019 Alternative 3 (no GPU verifier) with a margin and contradicts its Performance plan row labels; it also says that of the three size levers in the ledger, the Ligerito level 2 over the combined rows (queue item 34) is the one that moves `verify` as well: it removes the 1.14 GB of rows and all 17 verifier NTTs, 57% of the CPU budget, while IDEA-0002 removes the 2^22 interpolation step alone (−32% of the NTT term, −18% of the budget) and RFC-0021's `τ_A` 130 removes 5% ([E0-SUR12]).

**2. Recursion (NG-3) is priced for the first time, and the price is the hashing, not the arithmetic.** The arithmetic of Tessera's verifier is about 1.1×10^10 multiplication gates in layered, data-parallel shape (inner products, NTT butterflies, table MLEs), which GKR proves at about 122 ps per gate on an H100 ([Polyhedra24]): about 1.4 GPU-s, noise against a proof that takes hours (E0). The hashing is 1.4×10^7 BLAKE3 compressions at `τ_A` 155 (1.2×10^7 at 130); Flock proves 82k compressions per second per M4 Max core and 660k on ten ([BRW26]), so about 170 core-seconds or 20 s on a workstation, and the leanVM circuit prices a hash-based recursion at about 340 committed words per compression ([Kev26]), about 5×10^9 committed words for Tessera's column check, 0.2% of the 2.4×10^12 elements the proof already commits. The landscape's standing row ("as BLAKE3 in a Goldilocks AIR the recursion circuit would be as large as the proof") stands as a size statement and falls as a cost statement: a column-check recursion is cheap to prove on paper. What it costs is elsewhere: (i) a second proof system in the verifier's trusted base, exactly RFC-0013's reason for rejecting a ZK-STARK wrapper and the SP-6 concern RFC-0019 Alternative 3 raises for GPU drivers; `tv-core` would have to implement a second, Fiat–Shamir-transformed verifier from a second specification; (ii) a field mismatch: Flock's rate is over binary fields, Tessera's columns are Goldilocks, so either the circuit re-encodes 8-byte symbols bitwise or a Goldilocks BLAKE3 AIR is priced from scratch; (iii) the live mode cannot be compressed before its session ends (the challenges are the verifier's), so recursion serves the non-interactive mode and archival, which is where NG-3 placed it; (iv) the assurance target moves from "the verifier code" to "the circuit and the outer verifier", the layer the SP1 case shows is hardest to verify ([Gun26]). Accumulation (ARC, WARP; [BMNW24], [BCFW25]) does not remove the circuit, it shrinks what the circuit hashes per proof to a few Merkle openings, which matters at the epoch's 681 proofs, not for one. Stwo's move from a Cairo verifier to a fixed circuit (1 min → 3 s, [StarkWare26]) and SP1's normalize-compress tree ([SP1rec26]) say the circuit must be special-purpose; Tessera's verifier is already a fixed program, which helps. The reopening condition of RFC-0011 Alternative 2 and RFC-0013 ("recursion enters scope") is now a cost question with numbers, not a direction.

**3. Attestable's formal-verification claim does not exist in its primary sources.** Both pages re-read today make no mention of formal verification, machine-checked proofs, Lean, audits or open source ([Attestable26a], [Attestable26c]); the technical post names no proof system either. The landscape's Statement cell and the scoreboard's "Verifier: formal verification claimed, the bar to clear" are unsupported and should be corrected; the bar is Tessera's own NG-7, set against a field in which no hash-based SNARK verifier has been verified end to end.

**4. The state of verifier formalization is layered, and each layer has a different maturity.** (a) Protocol soundness: the sumcheck is mechanized in Isabelle ([GBS24]), FRI's round-by-round soundness in Lean in about six weeks with AI provers ([GMW25], [Hir26]), a fixed-statement zk-STARK end to end in Isabelle at 2^-137 ([Mar26]), Johnson-regime RS bounds in Lean ([DKT26], [ArkLib26]) which Draft RFC-0021 already imports; nothing for an interleaved Ligero-style test (Polygero's Lean-checked schedules [FS26] are the nearest), nothing for LogUp-GKR, and Fiat–Shamir is "future work" in ArkLib. (b) Statement encoding: StarkWare has twice proven that its AIR's satisfiability implies the computational claim ([AGLST22], [AGG+26]); Tessera's analogue is RFC-0009's uniqueness lemmas and RFC-0006's range analysis, which is where SP-3 lives and the only layer a vendor has finished. (c) Implementation: hax 0.4 now extracts safe Rust to Lean through Charon and Aeneas ([Cryspen26], [Aeneas26]); `tv-core` is inside the supported subset by construction (safe Rust, no `unwrap`, checked arithmetic), but the translation's fidelity is itself unverified and has documented gaps on the F* side ([Sym26]), and the SP1 effort shows that an unverified constraint extractor, scoping hypotheses and an unupdated proof after a fix turn "verified" into a facade in the critic's word ([Gun26]). The two routes of OQ-0019-1 are therefore not alternatives but a sequence: a Lean model of `docs/verifier/spec.md` tied to `tv-core` by differential tests first (route 2, the blueprint discipline of [LA25] and the predicate-per-step shape RFC-0019 already requires), and extraction (route 1) only for the leaf functions where Aeneas's subset and a kernel-checked equivalence are both available ([KCT26] did exactly this for FRI folding and Merkle inclusion). The audit evidence ([zkSecStwo25]) says what the tamper corpus must contain before any of this: every public input absorbed before the challenge it influences (INV-FS-1), every instance that joins a LogUp sum accounted for (RFC-0008), every prover-supplied length checked before a `zip` (RFC-0003), uniform challenges (RFC-0004). None of this changes a cost term; it changes the scoreboard's assurance row from a comparison with a claim to a plan with known prior art.

**Contested.** Whether constant-size proofs at Attestable's scale come from a FRI-family PCS or from recursion is unknowable from its pages. Whether Flock's binary-field rate transfers to Goldilocks column data is an open E0 question (a bitwise re-encoding multiplies the witness by 64; a Goldilocks BLAKE3 AIR is unpriced). Whether ARC-style accumulation covers an interleaved proximity test at all is unexamined.

## Candidate ideas

- **Parallel verifier.** Add `rayon` (already allowlisted) to `tv-core` and `tv-hash` over columns, combined rows and tables: `verify` about 13 s → under 1 s on 32 cores (E0); first killer: a dependency in the trusted base whose scheduler the fuzzing campaign and the determinism test (RFC-0019 'Testing Strategy') must cover; the result bytes must not depend on thread order.
- **Transfer-plus-check as the verify metric, with streamed verification.** Hash and check each opened column as it arrives (the chained digests make a column self-contained) so verification finishes within one transfer time and RAM stays at O(rows) per column: `verify` wall clock bounded by 17 s at 1 Gbit/s instead of transfer plus 13 s (E0); first killer: the TCE envelope's length-before-allocation discipline (RFC-0003) against a streaming decoder.
- **Ligerito level 2 over the combined rows (queue item 34), now also a `verify` idea.** Removes the 17 `Fp2` NTTs (−57% of the CPU budget) with the 1.14 GB (−53% of size) (E0, [E0-SUR12] over [SUR-0005](SUR-0005-commitments.md)); first killer: the second level's verifier is succinct only with coefficient-form rows (IDEA-0002) and its proximity analysis in the Johnson regime is unwritten.
- **Non-interactive compression of the column check (NG-3, priced).** A hash-based outer proof of the 1.4×10^7 column compressions and the 1.1×10^10 arithmetic gates: about 170 core-seconds of Flock-rate hashing plus about 1.4 GPU-s of GKR (E0), proof about 2 GB → tens of MiB; first killer: SP-6 and RFC-0013's objection (a second proof system and its specification enter the independent verifier), then the binary-versus-Goldilocks mismatch.
- **Epoch accumulation of proximity claims (ARC/WARP).** Fold the column-proximity claims of an epoch's proofs into one accumulator so one decision is hashed per epoch: `size` per proof and the ledger's cross-proof work (E0, unquantified); first killer: whether an interleaved Ligero-style test is an accumulable proximity claim at all.
- **Verifier blueprint before v1.0 (OQ-0019-1 route 2 first).** Write `docs/verifier/spec.md` as a Lean blueprint (theorem statements per check, in the shape of [LA25] and [ArkLib26]), tie it to `tv-core` by the tamper corpus and differential tests, import the sumcheck ([GBS24], ported) and the RFC-0021 bounds ([DKT26]) as the first two mechanized lemmas, and extract only leaf functions (field arithmetic, NTT, Merkle, BLAKE3 compression) through hax 0.4 where a kernel-checked equivalence is available ([KCT26]): the NG-7 row of the scoreboard; first killer: effort (SP1's 62-opcode chip layer took a year and reached 51) and the scoping trap [Gun26] documents.
- **Tamper-corpus items from the audit evidence.** Add to [07 §4](../../docs/spec/07-testing-strategy.md)'s corpus the two high findings of [zkSecStwo25] in Tessera's terms (an output claim not absorbed before the LogUp challenge; an optional instance left out of a LogUp sum) and the `zip`-length case: `bits` as assurance, no cost term; first killer: none, it is work for the `soundness-review` skill, not an idea note.
- Findings without a seed: [SGO+26], [LY26], [ACC+26] (landscape rows; different statements); [GLL26] (a statement caveat, recorded in the landscape); [BSB22], [StarkWare26], [SP1rec26] (shape, no transferable number); [Mar26], [AGLST22], [AGG+26], [Succinct25fv], [Sym26], [RustVer24] (inputs to the blueprint seed).
