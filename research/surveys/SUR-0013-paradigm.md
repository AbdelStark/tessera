## Method

**Frame.** The agenda's one question is five questions, and each touches the scoreboard rather than a unit cost. (a) *Approximate sumcheck* touches the statement row of [baseline.md §5](../baseline.md#5-scoreboard) (level 1 admits only systems that prove "the full statement") and bounded mode, whose answer today is [RFC-0017](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0017-scoring.md): a committed predictor prices the deployment's mismatch with the integer `D` in bits (`Û`) instead of tolerating it inside the circuit, and [RFC-0009](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0009-gadgets.md) Alternatives Considered rejects the tolerance-band softmax for the witness slack it leaves. (b) *Folding and accumulation* touch `size` and `verify` (the 2 GB / 300 s rows) and are fixed today by NG-3 ([00 §6](../../docs/spec/00-overview.md#6-non-goals-for-v1): no recursive compression in v1) and by [RFC-0010](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0010-proof-schedule.md)'s constant-depth stages against layer-by-layer GKR. (c) *TEE hybrids* and (d) *bit-exact recomputation* are the non-cryptographic rows of [landscape.md](../landscape.md#non-cryptographic-verification-competing-approaches) that the scoreboard's "beats every known protocol" must be measured against; today's answer is the source's §3 sentence ("TEE-based attestation costs under 2% but rests on unbroken silicon"; recomputation "neither hides weights ... nor survives an OC5 adversary"). (e) *New statements* touch NG-1 (MoE routing privacy, source §9 problem 2), NG-9 (training proofs, per-entry sampling rejected in source §4) and the ledger's queue items 28, 43, 48 and 51 (bounded-mode proof-aware `D` and shared-prefix proof units), which are priced against `Û` and the per-request `A·S` of [baseline.md §2](../baseline.md#2-the-cost-model).

**Search.** IACR ePrint and arXiv (cs.CR, cs.LG, cs.AR, cs.PF) by keyword and by author, from the agenda's seeds ("ePrint 2025/2152", "proof of inference TEE hybrid", "MoE routing privacy committed permutation proof") and from the branches each hit opened: "approximate sum-check" and its citing notes; "folding accumulation zkML", "Layered Sumcheck Accumulation"; "confidential computing H100 overhead", "GPU TEE attack", "attestation report measurement"; "bit-exact replay", "batch invariance", "RepOps", "TOPLOC", "DiFR", "TensorCommitments", "refereed delegation", "opML", "Proof of Sampling"; "zero-knowledge proof of training LLM", "zkPoT", "verifiable fine-tuning"; "zkMoE", "CryptoMoE"; "shared prefix KV cache proof". Primary sources first: the EUROCRYPT 2026 version of the approximate sum-check paper was read in full from the second author's page (the ePrint PDF sits behind a browser challenge on 2026-10-17); the Verde paper's body was read in full (PDF); ZKTorch's evaluation section, DiFR, TensorCommitments, Hollow-LLM, the quantized-supply-chain paper, the training-verification proposal and the fine-tuning protocol were read from their arXiv HTML (first 100,000 characters each); the rest were read as abstracts or landing pages, which the Findings say. The Deterministic Horizon thesis was searched for its "Collapse" section through its HTML source because its references are not on the abstract page. Every URL below was checked on 2026-10-17 unless the bibliography entry says otherwise. Done when each question had three primary sources: (a) has three ([BDGIKT25], [Zamir26], [RKM25]) plus two optimistic-verification papers that define the alternative; (b) has three ([CTK25], [APKP24], [Guo26], the last a `claim`); (c) has four measured ([NVIDIAcc23], [CCMH25], [WWS26], [Chen26]) and two hybrids ([Chan25], [EigenAI26]); (d) has eleven; (e) has eight. ## Synthesis

**Settled.**

1. *Approximate sumcheck is not a competitor to the integer statement at Tessera's soundness, and the reason is arithmetic, not taste.* [BDGIKT25]'s round-by-round soundness is `d/|S| + 8√(vd · δ/Δ)` over R (`2√(...)` over C) and completeness needs `δ ≥ d · 2^v · ε` for a sum over `{0,1}^v`. Solving for 100 bits non-interactive at `v = 41` (the 2^41 exp terms of a 16K/16K proof) and `d = 3` gives `Δ/δ ≥ 2^209`–`2^213` and `Δ/ε ≥ 2^252`–`2^256`; at `v = 14` (one softmax row) still `2^223`–`2^227`; the interactive bound for 64 live bits at `v = 41` is worse (`2^261`) because of its `2^v` factor ([E0-this]). A prover that certifies a sum to absolute error `Δ` must therefore carry a mantissa 220–260 bits longer than `Δ`, where Goldilocks carries 64 integer bits exactly: the paper's own "intermediate security" (two bits of precision per bit of soundness, plus one per variable) is the whole story. The paper confirms this reading by offering no application beyond a fully linear IOP and by stating that over finite fields the metric is trivial. [Zamir26] closes the other door: layerwise tolerances do not compose into an end-to-end bound, which is why a statement that tolerates error must bound it globally. Tessera already does exactly that in bounded mode: `Û` is a per-sequence code length under a committed predictor (RFC-0017 'Semantics'), valid for any predictor, with every prover freedom one-sided. The finding confirms 00 §3's exact mode, RFC-0017's predictor design and RFC-0009's rejection of the tolerance-band softmax; it moves [BDGIKT25] in the landscape from "a native route to floating-point statements" to "theory to track, closed at Tessera's sizes unless the `√(δ/Δ)` becomes linear or Remark 3's repetition conjecture is proven, which would remove the `2λ` but not the `v`".

2. *Bounded-mode fidelity is being solved from the deployment side, and the strongest result is Tessera's own quantization choice.* Three measurements fix the size of the problem: an unmodified serving stack is not self-consistent across batch sizes (1,000 greedy completions of Qwen3-235B give 80 distinct outputs, [He25]); making it batch-invariant costs 1.6–2.1× end to end on one GPU ([He25]) or 30–60% on matrix products and about 60% on Llama-3.1-1B ([Arun25]), and is not cross-hardware; fused-upcast kernels make linear layers bit-identical across Ampere, Ada and Hopper and are faster than the batch-invariant baseline ([ReproFuse26]). The result that matters most to Tessera is [ChenTR26]: rounding INT8 weight scales to powers of two, the choice RFC-0005 made for the integer model under RISK-7, makes two kernel libraries agree bitwise at every linear layer and byte-identical in generated tokens at 1.7B–14B, at a perplexity cost of 0.3–0.5% (the +157% penalty reported earlier was an artifact). The consequence for the statement: a deployment that serves the W8A8 `D` itself with power-of-two scales is bit-exact by construction, so it is exact mode (`Û = 0`) without the "modified serving stack" cost 00 §3 attributes to it, and the bounded-mode mismatch rate `r` of RFC-0017 becomes a property of the FP8/BF16 deployment's distance from `D`, which no source measures (SC-6 still owns it). [Karvonen25]'s Token-DiFR independently arrives at RFC-0017's sampler design (a per-request seed makes the tokens themselves evidence), which is prior art to cite in RFC-0017 'Sampler PRF'.

3. *Non-cryptographic verification sets a cost floor of 1–3× inference and none of it meets the statement.* The family now has measured members at every point: fingerprints at about 1% of inference ([Baser26] 0.97% prover, 0.12% verifier, 96% detection; TOPLOC 258 B per 32 tokens; SVIP), statistical re-execution with the weights ([Karvonen25], one forward pass), refereed delegation at ≥ 2× ([Arun25]), optimistic tolerance games at 0.3% runtime plus a challenge window ([YSL+26]), deterministic re-execution inside TEEs at about 2% ([EigenAI26], `claim`), sampled trace openings in milliseconds ([Anchuri26]). Every one needs the weights at a verifier or a trusted party, or accepts statistical detection, or both; none hides weights from the counterparty, none gives a soundness bound against an adversary who controls the hardware (OC5), and [LT26] shows why detection is the wrong primitive even for the weights file (benign drift 0.22% against tamper floors of 0.02–0.1%). The landscape's non-cryptographic table should carry two columns the scoreboard needs: "verifier needs weights" and "soundness: cryptographic / statistical / economic". The source's §3 sentence stands; the evidence behind it is now E1.

4. *TEEs are a composition, not a competitor, and their cost is 4–21%, not 2%.* H100 Confidential Compute alone costs 4–8% of throughput ([CCMH25]); with the host in TDX, the configuration a deployment runs, 17.7–21.1% of global token throughput and 22–28% of time to first token ([WWS26]); the CPU–GPU path is capped near 4 GB/s and HBM is not encrypted ([NVIDIAcc23]). What attestation certifies is the firmware, driver and microcode of the GPU, never the model or the kernels' semantics, so a TEE-plus-proof hybrid proves either a sample of executions ([Chan25], simulated) or the attestation itself (Marlin, Automata, via [Equilibrium25]) and inherits the TEE's trust for the rest. For Tessera the TEE's place is the one [Gong26] and [Attestable26b] point at: a workload proof binds tokens to `D`, not `D` to the compute it consumed (source §9 problem 7); an attestation of the serving CVM bound into the recorder (RFC-0015) would say that an attested stack produced the recorded stream, which is NG-8's coverage anchor. No reviewed break of the H100 GPU TEE was found on 2026-10-17; the published attacks target the host VM protection (WeSee on SEV-SNP) or are surveys.

5. *Folding and accumulation move `verify` and `size`, need homomorphic commitments, and remain the NG-3 route.* ZKTorch's parallel Mira accumulation proves LLaMA-2-7B in 2,645 s on a 64-thread Xeon with a 22.85 MB proof and a 100 s verifier over BN254/KZG ([CTK25]); Kaizen's recursion gives constant-size training proofs at 10^7 parameters ([APKP24]); Collapse's layered accumulation claims a 3.1× smaller recursive circuit by gate count only ([Guo26], `claim`). None is hash-based, none is post-quantum, and all of them target the verifier, which is where Tessera trails by two to three orders of magnitude (2 GB, 300 s). RFC-0010's constant-depth stages (layer-by-layer GKR rejected for depth) are confirmed in mechanism; the recursion question stays with the `verifier` survey and NG-3, where SUR-0005's finding (a Ligerito-style level 2 over the combined rows, about −48% of proof size) is the hash-based analogue.

6. *MoE routing privacy has no proof-system prior art; its mechanism has an MPC precedent.* No paper proves expert routing in zero knowledge with the route hidden: ZK-DeepSeek ([Wang25]) proves a component, VerInf ([Petrie26]) commits all 128 experts, the training proposal lists MoE verification as open problem OP-10 ([PNW26]), DiFR tests an MoE statistically ([Karvonen25]). CryptoMoE ([ZXHWL25]) is the precedent for the mechanism source §9 problem 2 proposes: fixed capacity `t` per expert, tokens beyond capacity dropped with confidence-aware selection, "minimum accuracy loss" on three MoE models at 2.8–3.5× the latency saving over a dense baseline. The witness bound the source states, `(1 + slack) × active experts` against 128×, is therefore plausible in accuracy terms but its slack is unmeasured for a Llama-4-class model, and the committed permutation (LogUp over (token, slot, value) tuples) is unpriced. NG-1 stands with a clearer first experiment.

7. *Training proofs stay out of scope; a checkpoint audit is already in scope.* Per-step proofs of training run minutes per iteration at 10^7 parameters ([APKP24], [LF25]) and the frontier proposal's 2–10% is an estimate with the zero-knowledge backpropagation proof marked its highest-risk milestone ([PNW26]); fine-tuning proofs publish the weights ([Akgul25]). NG-9 is confirmed. The statement that has moved is zkLLMPoT's ([LGW+26]): certify a committed checkpoint's objective on auditor-chosen sequences by a forward pass, independent of the training run. That is Tessera's statement with `Û` read as what RFC-0017 says it is, a code length: with a predictor `q = p̂` (the mixture's `ε = 1` limit) `Û` on auditor-chosen sequences is the committed `D`'s next-token loss in bits. Tessera would certify a 405B checkpoint's loss at the cost of a teacher-forced forward-pass proof, four orders of magnitude below any per-step training proof, with weights hidden.

8. *Shared prefixes have one precedent, within a transcript.* zkAgent ([Wang26a]) proves a multi-step transcript in one causal-mask forward pass (1,047× over step-by-step proving on GPT-2); that is the batching every teacher-forced proof performs, Tessera's included. No source amortizes a prefix shared across requests (system prompts, retrieved documents). The count for queue item 51 is elementary ([E0-this]): for `R` requests sharing a prefix of `P` tokens in context `S`, the prefix's linear work is a fraction `P/S` of each request's and its attention scores a fraction `(P/S)²` (12.5% and 1.6% at `P = 2K, S = 16K`; 75% and 56% at `P = 12K`), so the saving is first-order only for retrieval-style prefixes, and it costs a cross-proof-unit binding of the prefix's advice (RFC-0011 streams per proof) and publishes that the requests share a prefix (sequence lengths are already public).

**Contested or open.**

9. *The TEE overhead number*: 4–8% ([CCMH25], CC alone, 2025) against 17.7–21.1% ([WWS26], CC under TDX, 2026) and "under 2%" (source §3; [EigenAI26] for deterministic kernels, a different quantity). The configurations differ (host VM confidential or not); the landscape should carry both with their conditions.

10. *Approximate sumcheck's Remark 3* (parallel repetition boosts Fiat–Shamir security linearly without more precision) is a conjecture; if proven it removes the factor 2 in `2λ` but leaves `v + λ`, still `2^140`-plus precision at Tessera's sizes ([E0-this]).

11. *Bit-exact recomputation across GPU generations* is claimed for linear layers ([ReproFuse26]) and by emulation ([Cankaya26]) but [EigenAI26] measures 0% cross-architecture agreement for a whole model and [He25] disclaims hardware invariance; attention and normalization kernels are the gap.

**Standing of the RFCs.** 00 §3 (exact and bounded modes) and RFC-0017 (predictor, sampler PRF, one-sided freedom) are confirmed by every source that touches them, and gain two citations ([Karvonen25] for the seed, [ChenTR26] for the integer deployment). RFC-0009's tolerance-band rejection is confirmed by [Zamir26]. RFC-0005's power-of-two scales (RISK-7) gain a measured fidelity cost (0.3–0.5% perplexity at 1.7B–14B, Qwen3). NG-1, NG-3, NG-9 stand; NG-8 gains a mechanism (attestation as coverage anchor). No RFC is contradicted.

## Candidate ideas

- **Exact mode by an integer-exact deployment** ([ChenTR26], [ReproFuse26], [He25] with RFC-0005 'Fidelity evaluation' and RFC-0017): serve the W8A8 `D` with power-of-two scales on kernels that agree bitwise, so the production path is exact mode (`Û = 0`) and the "modified serving stack" cost of 00 §3 is the measured 0.3–0.5% perplexity and the kernel's throughput; moves `statement` and `Û` (SC-6: 0.88 → 0 against the 0.1 target), no `μ`; killer: [ChenTR26] shows two kernel libraries agreeing, not two GPU generations, and attention and normalization kernels are outside its claim, so the v0.4 harness must measure byte-identity of whole-model outputs across H100 SKUs first.
- **`Û` as a checkpoint-audit certificate** ([LGW+26]'s statement, RFC-0017 'Predictors'): add a `Loss` predictor (`q = p̂`, the mixture at `ε = 1`) so `Û` over auditor-chosen sequences is the committed `D`'s next-token loss in bits with weights hidden; moves `statement` breadth at zero `μ` (one teacher-forced proof) against per-step training proofs four orders of magnitude costlier; killer: the auditor's sequences must be chosen after `R_W` is committed (RFC-0015 frame order) and the `+1` smoothing of `p̂` biases the loss by `log2(1 + V/Z)` per token, which must be reported as a bound, not a value.
- **Shared-prefix proof units** (queue item 51; [Wang26a] as the only precedent; count in [E0-this]): commit a shared prefix's advice once and bind it into every proof unit that shares it; moves per-request `C_lin`, `K_lin` by `P/S` and `A·S` by `(P/S)²` (75% and 56% at `P = 12K, S = 16K`, 12.5% and 1.6% at `P = 2K`); killer: the cross-unit opening of a commitment RFC-0011 streams and frees per proof, and zero knowledge of the fact that requests share a prefix.
- **Fixed-capacity expert slots priced by CryptoMoE's drop rate** (NG-1; [ZXHWL25], source §9 problem 2): expert witness `(1 + slack) × active` against 128× ([Petrie26]), with the committed permutation as one LogUp table over (token, slot, value); moves `statement` (NG-1) and `C_lin`; killer: the drop rate and accuracy at capacity `t` are published for ≤ 16B MoE models under MPC, not for Llama-4-Maverick, and the permutation's `K_lin` is unpriced.
- **Attestation as a compute-coverage anchor** (NG-8; [NVIDIAcc23], [Attestable26b], [Gong26]): bind the serving CVM's H100 attestation report into the recorder frames of RFC-0015 so the proven stream is tied to an attested stack; moves `statement` (coverage, source §9 problem 7) at the deployment's 4–21% TEE cost ([CCMH25], [WWS26]), no `μ`; killer: attestation measures firmware, driver and microcode, not the model, and trusts silicon, so it can never replace a conjunct, only annotate one.
- **Two scoreboard columns for the non-cryptographic table** ("verifier needs weights", "soundness: cryptographic / statistical / economic"; [Baser26] Table 1, [Arun25], [Karvonen25], [YSL+26]): bookkeeping that makes the 1–3× cost floor and its missing properties explicit; killer: none, it is a landscape edit.
- No seed from [BDGIKT25], [Zamir26], [RKM25], [YSL+26], [PNW26] (approximate statements: closed by the precision count and the non-composability note; the integer route is confirmed), from [CTK25], [APKP24], [Guo26] (homomorphic-commitment recursion, NG-3), from [Chan25], [EigenAI26], [Chen26], [Conway24], [ZWTLMP24], [Ong25], [SLZJZ24], [Anchuri26], [LT26], [Cankaya26], [Equilibrium25] (non-cryptographic rows: landscape only), or from [Akgul25], [LF25], [Merbaum26], [Andreoletti26] (different statements).

## Staleness triggers

- A follow-up to [BDGIKT25] with a soundness bound linear in `δ/Δ`, a proof of its Remark 3, or any implementation applied to a GKR layer or a matrix product with stated precision.
- A measurement of whole-model byte-identity across H100 SKUs for a power-of-two W8A8 Llama-3.1 deployment (the v0.4 fidelity harness, SC-6), or a cross-generation extension of [ChenTR26] or [ReproFuse26] to attention and normalization.
- A proof system that hides MoE expert routes (any construction), or a published drop rate at fixed capacity for a Llama-4-class model.
- The full text of zkLLMPoT ([LGW+26]) with hardware and end-to-end numbers, or a Tessera-side `Loss` predictor in RFC-0017.
- A reviewed break of the H100 GPU TEE, or NVIDIA documentation that changes what the attestation report measures.
- Peer review or an independent reproduction of [Baser26], [EigenAI26] or [Chan25]; GPU numbers for RepOps on an H100 ([Arun25]).
- Resolution of [PNW26]'s OP-3 (zero-knowledge backpropagation at scale) in either direction.
