## Synthesis

**Settled.**

1. *Libra-style masking is the cheapest known zero knowledge for a sumcheck, and it is optimal in size.* A sumcheck over `ℓ` variables reveals `O(ℓ)` field elements, so a mask with `O(ℓ)` random coefficients (`g = a_0 + Σ g_i(x_i)`) suffices and no smaller mask can ([Xie24] §2.4.1, "sufficient and optimal"). Every later hash-based or lattice-based construction keeps this shape: ZOOK's per-round short masks in a WHIR-style IOPP ([CFW26], "negligible overhead", `(1 + o(1))×` at large sizes by the implementer's preliminary plots), LatticeBlindFold's "Libra-style polynomial masking" ([DallAva26]), zkComposer's per-layer masked sumchecks ([SGO+26]). The alternative family, hide the round messages inside homomorphic commitments (Hyrax 2018, BlindFold 2026), costs little in Jolt (+3 KB, 0–10% prover, [a16z26zk], [JoltBF26]) but needs Pedersen, Dory or HyperKZG and is therefore not post-quantum and not hash-based; Jolt's own documentation ties ZK to the Dory backend and rejects it with Akita. For Tessera, RFC-0013 Alternative 2 ("commit every round message") stays rejected on these grounds, and Alternative 1 (a ZK-STARK wrapper) is the recursion route NG-3 excludes.

2. *At Tessera's scale zero knowledge costs about 0.1% of `μ` and nothing measurable in proof size (E0).* The counts in [E0-this] put the mask rows at 0.09% of `μ(16K)`, the masking polynomials below 10^-5, the blinding rows at 0.001 of `μ` even after the #239 doubling, and the weight-budget shortening at +0.02% at most. The task statement's premise that zero knowledge hides inside the glue factor (15% of `μ`) is therefore a misattribution by two orders of magnitude; the glue is sumcheck rounds and `eq` tables (SUR-0003, IDEA-0008), and the cost model should carry `zk ≈ 1.001` as its own line so that the sensitivity table never credits a zero-knowledge change with a glue-sized saving. Nothing in the corpus measures zero-knowledge overhead on a GPU; the only measured figures are CPU and indirect (Virgo's omitted masks, +90% of a zkVPD prover, [RV24]; ZOOK's plots without numbers, [ocdbytes26a]).

3. *The hiding rules of RFC-0011/RFC-0013 match production Ligero practice, and the #239 defect has a published mirror image.* Longfellow ([Longfellow26]) uses `R = nreq` random cells per block (Tessera's `P ≥ τ`), three blinding rows added with coefficient 1 to the revealed combinations (Tessera's `Q_g`), and blinds in the subfield when, and only when, the witnesses and the revealed combinations are in the subfield. Tessera's combined rows are `Fp2`-linear combinations, so their blinding rows must span `Fp2`: two `Fp` rows as one virtual row, the repair of RFC-0021 'Binding the blinding claims'. The same small-field mismatch (base-field randomizers against extension-field challenges, quotient splits) is what [HK24] found in Plonky2, RISC Zero and Triton, with RISC Zero conceding "there is no solution available yet" in July 2024 ([RZ24]); Virgo's code shipped without its masking polynomial ([RV24]); Orion's zero-knowledge revision broke soundness ([dHS25]). Four of the best-known hash-based provers failed on zero knowledge in exactly the mechanical details RFC-0013 fixes by hand, which confirms RISK-6's gate (a written simulator argument reviewed before implementation) and the distinguisher suite of 07 §7, and argues for adding to that suite the structural tests these failures suggest: a mask drawn from a proper subfield of the message, a mask that vanishes at a revealed point, a mask omitted by the implementation.

4. *Composition across stages is understood, in three layers.* (a) Classical: auxiliary-input zero knowledge is closed under sequential composition ([Gol02], [GO94]); statistical simulation errors add, which is what RFC-0013 step 6 does when it sums the degenerate events. (b) GKR-shaped recursion: [CFS17] §11 gives the only formal composition for the GKR recursion, through one random linear combination of all claims about a vertex (RFC-0013's stacked layers and RFC-0010's batching) and one random low-degree extension per vertex that keeps the revealed child evaluations uniform (RFC-0013's masked extensions); Libra's Theorems 2.4.2–2.4.3 give the simulator for the GKR-plus-zkVPD composition in the trapdoor-commitment model. (c) Oracle-sharing reductions: Tessera's stages are not independent runs; they share one advice commitment, one seed and the claims that cross stage boundaries, so the whole proof is one IOP, and [CFW26] is the first definition of honest-verifier zero knowledge for interactive oracle reductions that composes by construction, with an explicit error per reduction (`(t² + t)/(2|F|)` per code switch in the implementation). Honest-verifier suffices for the non-interactive mode because the BCS compiler preserves zero knowledge ([BCS16]); the live mode's malicious verifier is handled by the coin-tossed seed (challenges uniform) and by column hiding "for every choice within the budget" (`P ≥ τ`), which is where RFC-0013 adds to the honest-verifier literature. What the literature does not supply is a reusable theorem for RFC-0013's exact shape; the simulator argument of RISK-6 has to be written, and its structure is the one RFC-0013 already outlines: simulate the commitment once (random rows), then each stage in order with the previous stages' simulated outputs as auxiliary input, with every cross-stage value masked.

**Contested or open.**

5. *BlindFold's prover cost*: 0% ([a16z26zk]) against 5–10% ([JoltBF26] mirror); irrelevant to Tessera but recorded for the landscape row of Jolt.

6. *ZOOK's overhead* is "negligible" in the abstract and `(1 + o(1))×` in an implementer's preliminary plots with three unreviewed deviations from the paper; the number to wait for is the camera-ready's table.

7. *OQ-0013-1 (packing mask rows)*: no source packs masks across tensors, but every source proves the condition that decides it: a mask with as many independent degrees of freedom as revealed values, with linearly independent evaluation vectors ([CFS17] one extension per vertex for all claims; Libra's constant-size `R_i` for four values). Several small tensors can therefore share one mask row of length `L` whenever their revealed points are distinct and `Σ e_i ≤ L`, at the same failure probability `(Σ e_i)²/|Fp2|`. The gain is bounded by the mask rows' own cost: at most 0.3% of committed rows and 0.09% of `μ` (E0), and the small tensors it targets are the smallest share of the 8×10^9 elements. Recommendation: close OQ-0013-1 as "admissible, not worth a label change".

8. *OQ-0013-2 (tree levels)*: [GOS24]'s general warning applies: a mask multiplied by a factor that vanishes at the revealed point hides nothing, and Tessera's tree-level points `(0 ‖ r')`, `(1 ‖ r')` have a Boolean coordinate where `Z` vanishes. The fix the sources support is a mask indexed by the Boolean prefix with `Z` taken over the random coordinates only (two mask values per level per tensor, or one mask row `R_V[b, x_col]`), plus `zk-sum`/`zk-rho` labels for the levels' round messages; the cost is below 10^6 elements per proof (E0). The same construction is the prerequisite for a zero-knowledge Hobbit tile fold, whose per-tile evaluations `f_i(v)` sit at points whose tile coordinates are Boolean (SUR-0003, [PP25]).

**Standing of RFC-0013.** Confirmed in mechanism by every source (Alternatives 1–3 stay rejected; the masks are optimal; the hiding rules match production Ligero); contradicted in one detail already filed (#239, the field of the blinding row); and its cost table is right but unpriced in the cost model.

## Candidate ideas

- **Explicit `zk` term in the cost model** (`zk ≈ 1.001` from [E0-this], listed as its own line beside glue): moves accounting, not `μ`; gain is a correct sensitivity table (the glue's −7.4% stops being attributed to zero knowledge); killer: none beyond a baseline.md edit; it is a correction, not an experiment.
- **Zero-knowledge Hobbit tile fold** ([PP25] with [CFS17] §11 and the Vega/BlindFold "random instance first" shape, [VegaDocs26]): fold one random satisfying tile instance before the `N/B` real tiles as a one-time pad for the accumulated instance, mask the folded sumcheck and the tile-variable sumcheck Libra-style, and reveal the `2 · N/B` tile evaluations through prefix-indexed masked extensions; moves `stream` (the −28% / −42% of `μ` at 16K / 128K SUR-0003 found, conditional) at a zero-knowledge cost of about `1/346` of the fold work plus `N/B` mask values per tensor per level (E0); killer: Hobbit's fold messages must be enumerated from the paper and each shown masked or determined (the paper has no zero-knowledge treatment); then the independence condition at `e_V = N/B = 2,771` points at 128K (`2^-105`, fine).
- **Prefix-indexed masked extensions and masked round messages for tree levels** (closes OQ-0013-2; [GOS24] warning, [CFS17] per-vertex extension, Libra `R_i` with `Z` over the random coordinates only): `R_V[b, x_col]` per level and `zk-sum`/`zk-rho` labels per level sumcheck; moves `zk` (correctness) and `size` by under 10^6 elements per proof (E0); killer: the label table freezes at `tessera-protocol/1` (v0.3), and the last-round degree raise of each level's masked extension must fit the engine's degree bound.
- **Close OQ-0013-1 as admissible but not worth it**: packing small tensors' masks into shared rows is sound under the independence condition every source proves ([CFS17], [Xie24]) and saves at most 0.3% of committed rows and 0.09% of `μ` (E0); killer: the saving is below the noise of the glue factor; first action is a sentence in RFC-0013, not an experiment.
- **Zero-knowledge conformance tests from the published failures** ([RV24] omitted masks, [HK24]/[RZ24] base-field randomizers, [dHS25] revised ZK breaking soundness, #239 `Im(u'_g)`): add to 07 §7 structural distinguishers (mask in a proper subfield of the message, mask vanishing at a revealed point, mask absent) that are red on today's RFC-0013 text and green after #239's repair; moves `zk` assurance, no `μ`; killer: statistical tests see structural leaks only, never a `2^-100` simulation gap, so the written simulator argument (RISK-6) stays the gate.
- **A written simulator argument shaped as composable IOR zero knowledge** ([CFW26] definition, [BCS16] for the non-interactive mode, [Gol02] sequential hybrid for the stage order, RFC-0013's coin toss and `P ≥ τ` for the live verifier): one commitment simulated once, one reduction per stage with an explicit error term, every cross-stage value masked; moves `zk` (the v0.4 entry gate of RISK-6); killer: the honest-verifier definition does not cover the live verifier's free column choices, which must be argued separately as RFC-0013 'Malicious verifier' does.
- No seed from [JM26], [CDGH24], [SGO+26], [WTSTW18], [DallAva26], [JoltAtlas26] and the BlindFold sources: different models or discrete-log and lattice commitments the hash-based design cannot use; they are recorded for the landscape.
