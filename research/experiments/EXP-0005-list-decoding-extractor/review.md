## 3. Step 3 (every group bound to Q*): HOLDS

- `A ∩ B_g ⊆ Agr(w_g, c_g·Q*)`: for `j ∈ A` every row of `Q*` equals `U` (item 1's iff), so `(c_g·Q*)[j] = (c_g·U)[j]`; for `j ∈ B_g`, `w_g[j] = (c_g·U)[j]`. Both are polynomials of degree ≤ D = k' − 1 (the shape check RFC-0011:83 forces `w_g = enc(u_g) + Z_H·M_g` with `u_g` of `k` values and `M_g` of `P_A` coefficients), so if distinct they agree on ≤ D points: `|A ∩ B_g| ≤ D`, probability ≤ `(D/n)^τ = ρ₋^τ ≤ (ρ')^τ` (2^-130.0 at τ = 130, 2^-130.05 at τ_W = 68).
- Order: combined rows absorbed at order 8, queries at order 10 (RFC-0014:84-86), INV-PROTO-3 (RFC-0014:133); NI queries are squeezed after `query-pow`, whose 2^20 retries are charged as `g_query`. Queries are uniform with replacement (RFC-0011:84); the event "draw i lands in A ∩ B_g" is per draw, so repeated indices cost nothing. The prover cannot make `w_g` depend on the queries. If the prover answers group g from another list element `Q'`, either `c_g·Q' ≠ c_g·Q*` (caught at `(D/n)^τ`) or `c_g·Q' = c_g·Q*` as polynomials, in which case the claim is bound to `Q*` anyway. No union over G is needed: all τ draws must land in the first inconsistent group's set. RFC-0013's blinding row only changes `c_g` to `c_g + e_Q`, still public.

## 5. Step 5 (Fp coefficients) and step 6 (hiding, grinding): step 5 HOLDS, hiding HOLDS, grinding WRONG in NI mode

- Frobenius argument: correct. It needs `D = 7·L ⊆ Fp` (RFC-0011:45), `U` Fp-valued (opened columns are `Vec<Vec<Fp>>`, RFC-0014:64, so the format enforces it), and `|A| ≥ (1−γ)n ≥ k'` (same inequality as `hkThreshold`).
- Hiding: τ_A = 130 ≤ P_A = 160, 162 ≤ 200; weights are governed by the per-epoch budget (RFC-0012:79), fewer columns per proof only lengthens the epoch; ξ is public; RFC-0013's simulator is unchanged. The weight commitment has the same shape (rows (ℓ, o_hi) plus `B_W` blinding rows, mask `P_W`, blinded fold as combined row), so steps 2, 3 and 5 carry over; step 4 does not (item 4).
- Grinding: RFC-0014:109 places `seed-pow` before the seed squeeze (order 3) and `query-pow` before the queries (order 10). `pcs/advice/gamma`, `pcs/advice/xi`, `pcs/weights/{T}/xi` are squeezed at order 7, after the prover's tree and stage messages (orders 5-6). A stage's final evaluations are prover-sent values constrained only by the stage's final check (RFC-0010:112: "the final evaluations are as many as the instances' relations list, and the final check holds"), one equation on several values, so the prover re-rolls (γ, ξ) by re-hashing a short transcript suffix, at no proof-of-work cost and without touching the seed. EXP step 6 ("squeezed from the transcript after the seed, so a retry costs the 20-bit seed-pow") and RFC-0021:60 ("`+ g_seed`" on `log2(|Fp2|/E₀)`) are wrong. ni-100's field term is therefore 80.41 one-shot bits (η = 0.002) against a 100-bit target, and no admissible η reaches 100 (90.3 at η = 0.02 per EXP-0003). The `1/q'` and `G·L·s_max/q'` terms are re-rollable the same way but stay above 107 bits. Fix: squeeze the `xi` labels immediately after `seed` (no prover message in between; ξ depends only on the public layout, and the MCA exceptional set depends only on `U`, fixed in Round1, so an early ξ costs the argument nothing), or add a 20-bit proof-of-work before the order-7 squeeze. Live mode is one-shot per session (INV-PROTO-1), as claimed.

## Overall verdict

Argument fails at step 4 (RFC-0021 step 3) because the blinding-row claim `q_g` of RFC-0013:81 is sent in `advice_combined` (RFC-0014:58), absorbed after `pcs/advice/gamma`, so the prover solves for `q_g` and the evaluation check binds no claim; the weight opening (RFC-0012:53) has no batching challenge and admits the shift `(v_T + Δ, q_T − Δ)` outright. Independently, step 6's grinding claim is wrong in non-interactive mode: ξ at order 7 is re-rollable through the stages' final evaluations at no proof-of-work cost, so ni-100's field term is 80.4 bits, not 100.4. Restricted to the non-hiding protocol the EXP actually models (RFC-0011 steps 4-8, live mode), the argument holds with these corrections: the list bound must cite `closePolynomialSet_finite_and_ncard_le_pairwiseJohnson` (or be stated as a derivation from `jetDegree_le` and `specialization_sound`), `hkThreshold` must be justified by the numeric margin, the `LineExactAgreementBound` glue must cite `Probability.lean:50-62`, and one ρ convention must be fixed. By the EXP's own pre-registered criterion ("inconclusive: the argument closes only under a further protocol change (a reordering of messages …)"), the outcome for the protocol RFC-0021 targets is `inconclusive`, not `validated`, until `q_g` and ξ are reordered.
