## Hypothesis

With independent uniform `Fp2` proximity coefficients in place of `eq(ξ*, row)`, the opening protocol of RFC-0011 (steps 4 to 8) stays sound when its proximity test is accounted in the Johnson regime: whenever the verifier accepts with probability above

`ε_total = ε_MCA + 1/q' + G · L · s_max / q' + (1 − γ)^τ + (ρ')^τ`

there is one codeword matrix `Q*`, among the codeword matrices `γ`-close to the committed matrix, whose rows have `Fp` coefficients and whose messages satisfy every evaluation claim of every group. Unique decoding (one close matrix) is replaced by list binding (several close matrices) plus consistency (every claim bound to the same one), and the only new term of the soundness budget's proximity row is the mutual-correlated-agreement error `ε_MCA`.

## Success criterion

- `validated`: every step of the argument rests on a cited primary statement, and the extra terms beyond `(1 − γ)^τ + ε_MCA` (the `1/q'`, `G · L · s_max/q'` and `(ρ')^τ` terms) stay below 2^-100 at the `live-64` and `ni-100` parameters, so that the budget row changes only by `ε_MCA` and the `τ` values of EXP-0003 stand.
- `refuted`: a step needs a property the primary sources do not provide (mutual correlated agreement for the eq generator, a list bound that grows with the number of rows, an exact-agreement form that is not available), or an extra term exceeds 2^-64.
- `inconclusive`: the argument closes only under a further protocol change (a reordering of messages, an extra pass over the rows, a per-claim combined row).

Pre-registration disclosure: the author sketched the shape of the argument (which agreement set the queries must land in) before writing this criterion; the criterion fixes which sources count and the admissible size of the extra terms, which had not been computed. This is a soundness argument written by one agent for review under the `soundness-review` skill, not a formal proof; the RFC that promotes IDEA-0012 must carry it as its security argument and a reviewer must read it adversarially.

## Method

- Setting, from the specification: committed matrix `U` of `M` non-zero `Fp`-valued rows, each meant to be a codeword of `C = RS[Fp2, D, k']` on the coset `D = 7·L` of size `n` (RFC-0011 'Encoding': `g = f + Z_H · m`, degree below `k' = k + P_A`); column digests and a Merkle root (RFC-0011 'Column digests and root'); the opening of steps 4 to 8 with the group coefficients `c_g(row) = Σ_t γ_t · eq(pt_t, row)` (step 2), proximity coefficients `ξ_row` (step 3, changed to independent uniform `Fp2`), the combined rows `u_g, M_g, u_ξ, M_ξ` (steps 4 and 5), the evaluation checks (step 6), `τ` uniform column queries with replacement (step 7) and the column checks (step 8). Per RFC-0014 'Transcript schedule' the batching challenges (`pcs/advice/gamma`) and `ξ` (`pcs/advice/xi`) are squeezed (order 7) before the combined rows are absorbed (order 8), so the prover chooses its combined rows knowing both.
- Statements used, read in the primary sources on 2026-10-16: [ArkLib26] `exists_affine_exceptionalSet_full_agreement_of_exactLine` (`LineToAffine.lean`: outside a set of seeds of density at most `B/(|F| − 1)`, every polynomial of degree below `k'` agreeing with the combination on at least `n(1 − radius)` coordinates decomposes as the same combination of codewords, and agrees with the combined word exactly where every codeword agrees with its row), with `B = johnsonExceptionCount` from `exists_johnson_line_exactCorrelatedPair_ceil` (`Johnson/Agreement.lean`) at radius `γ = 1 − √ρ' − η`; the Johnson interpolation certificate of the same file ("a nonzero ordinary equation whose roots include every candidate polynomial above the agreement threshold"), whose candidate degree `μ = johnsonMu` (`FiniteBounds.lean`) bounds the number of codewords agreeing with a word on at least `A` points; the Schwartz–Zippel lemma; unique interpolation of a polynomial of degree below `k'` from `k'` values.
- Steps of the argument: (1) scale the linear generator (every `ξ_row` uniform) to the affine one of [ArkLib26] (first coefficient 1), losing `1/q'`; (2) from the `ξ` column checks, either the queries catch the prover (`(1 − γ)^τ`) or the sent `w_ξ` is a codeword `γ`-close to `ξ · U`, which the exact-agreement statement decomposes as `ξ · Q*` for a codeword matrix `Q*` whose agreement set with `U` is exactly `A = Agr(w_ξ, ξ · U)`; (3) for every group `g`, show that the sent `w_g` equals `c_g · Q*` as a polynomial or the queries catch the prover with probability `1 − (ρ')^τ`, using only that the queried columns must lie in `A`; (4) show that the evaluation checks then bind every claim to `Q*`'s messages up to `G · L · s_max/q'`, `L ≤ μ` the list size, `G` the number of groups and `s_max` the largest number of claims in a group; (5) show `Q*`'s rows have `Fp` coefficients; (6) check that the hiding argument of RFC-0013 and the grinding of RFC-0014 are untouched.
- Numbers: the extra terms at `live-64` and `ni-100` with the `η` of EXP-0003 (`μ` from the recipe; `G = 16` from RFC-0011 'Sizes'; `s_max = 2^8`, a generous bound on claims per group; `τ` from EXP-0003), computed by [terms.py](terms.py) and recorded in [results/terms.txt](results/terms.txt).

## Results

### The argument

Notation. `Agr(a, b) = {j : a_j = b_j}` for words `a, b ∈ Fp2^n`; a word is `γ`-close to the code when some codeword agrees with it on at least `(1 − γ) n` coordinates. For a coefficient vector `z ∈ Fp2^M` and a matrix `X` with `M` rows, `z · X = Σ_row z_row X_row`. The prover's combined rows define the codewords `w_g := enc(u_g) + Z_H · M_g` and `w_ξ := enc(u_ξ) + Z_H · M_ξ` on `D` (each a polynomial of degree below `k'`). The column check of step 8.3 at a queried column `j` reads `w_g[j] = (c_g · U)[j]` for every `g` and `w_ξ[j] = (ξ · U)[j]`, where `U` is the committed matrix (by the collision resistance of the column digests and the Merkle root, RFC-0004, the opened symbols are `U`'s; that term is the budget's hash row). Write `A := Agr(w_ξ, ξ · U)` and `B_g := Agr(w_g, c_g · U)`. A queried column passes every check exactly when it lies in `A ∩ ⋂_g B_g`; the `τ` queries are uniform and independent (drawn with replacement, RFC-0011 step 7).

**Step 1, the generator.** [ArkLib26]'s `AffineSpaceGenerator F s` combines `s + 1` words with coefficients `(1, x_1, …, x_s)`, `x` uniform. RFC-0011 draws every `ξ_row` uniform. When `ξ_1 ≠ 0`, `ξ · U = ξ_1 · (U_1 + Σ_{i ≥ 2} (ξ_i / ξ_1) U_i)` with the ratios uniform and independent, and multiplication by the non-zero scalar `ξ_1` maps codewords to codewords and preserves every agreement set, so every statement below about the affine combination holds for `ξ · U` with `w_ξ` replaced by `ξ_1^{-1} w_ξ`. The event `ξ_1 = 0` has probability `1/q'`.

**Step 2, the `ξ` check pins one matrix.** Apply `exists_affine_exceptionalSet_full_agreement_of_exactLine` to the code of degree bound `k'` on `D`, dimension `s = M − 1`, radius `γ`, with the line bound `B = E₀ = johnsonExceptionCount(n, D, ⌈(√ρ₋ + η) n⌉, η)` supplied by `exists_johnson_line_exactCorrelatedPair_ceil` (its hypotheses `1 ≤ D ≤ n − 2`, `η > 0`, `√ρ₋ + η ≤ 1` hold at every parameter set). The theorem's thresholds hold: `⌈n (1 − γ)⌉ = ⌈(√ρ₋ + η) n⌉` is the agreement threshold `A_thr`, and `k' ≤ n (1 − γ)` since `n (1 − γ) = (√ρ₋ + η) n > ρ₋ n = D`. Conclusion: outside an exceptional set of coefficient vectors of density at most `E₀/(q' − 1) =: ε_MCA`, every polynomial of degree below `k'` that agrees with the combined word on at least `(1 − γ) n` coordinates is the same combination of codeword polynomials `P₀_row`, and agrees with the combined word exactly on `⋂_row Agr(P₀_row, U_row)`.

Two cases for the prover's `w_ξ`. (a) `|A| < (1 − γ) n`: every query lands in `A` with probability below `1 − γ`, so all `τ` pass with probability below `(1 − γ)^τ`. (b) `|A| ≥ (1 − γ) n` and `ξ` outside the exceptional set: `w_ξ = ξ · Q*` for a matrix `Q*` of codewords (the `P₀_row`, scaled back by `ξ_1`) with `⋂_row Agr(Q*_row, U_row) = A` exactly. So `Q*` is a codeword matrix `γ`-close to `U`, and `A` is precisely the set of columns on which every row of `Q*` agrees with `U`. Nothing in this step depends on the number of rows: the affine-space statement holds in every dimension at the same `E₀/(q' − 1)`.

**Step 3, every group is bound to the same matrix.** Fix a group `g`. The word `c_g · Q*` is a codeword and agrees with `c_g · U` on all of `A`, since `Q*` and `U` agree row by row on those columns. Suppose `w_g ≠ c_g · Q*` as polynomials. Both have degree below `k'`, so `|Agr(w_g, c_g · Q*)| ≤ k' − 1`. For `j ∈ A ∩ B_g`, `w_g[j] = (c_g · U)[j] = (c_g · Q*)[j]`, so `A ∩ B_g ⊆ Agr(w_g, c_g · Q*)` and `|A ∩ B_g| ≤ k' − 1 < ρ' n`. A query can pass both the `ξ` check and the `g` check only inside `A ∩ B_g`, so all `τ` queries pass with probability at most `((k' − 1)/n)^τ < (ρ')^τ`. The bound needs nothing about `c_g`: it is public and may be chosen by anyone before the prover's messages, and the same bound covers every group at once (if any group is inconsistent, the queries must land inside that group's set). Hence, except with probability `(ρ')^τ`, `w_g = c_g · Q*` for every `g`.

**Step 4, the claims bind to `Q*`'s messages.** Every codeword of degree below `k' = k + P_A` decomposes uniquely as `f + Z_H · m` with `deg f < k` (`f` is the remainder modulo `X^k − 1`) and `deg m < P_A`; its message is `f` on `H`, which is the codeword's own values on `H` since `Z_H` vanishes there. Let `q*_row` be the message of `Q*_row` and `Ã*` the multilinear extension of the matrix of messages in RFC-0011's layout. The message of `w_g = Σ_t γ_t (eq(pt_t) · Q*)` is `Σ_t γ_t Σ_row eq(pt_t, row) q*_row`, so the verifier's check of step 6, `Σ_t γ_t v_t = ⟨u_g, eq(col_g)⟩`, reads `Σ_t γ_t (v_t − Ã*(pt_t ‖ col_g)) = 0`. The claimed values `v_t` are absorbed before `pcs/advice/gamma` is squeezed; the prover, however, chooses `Q*` (through `w_ξ`) after seeing the `γ_t`, among the codeword matrices `γ`-close to `U`, a set `List_γ(U)` fixed by `U`. For one fixed `Q ∈ List_γ(U)` and one group with some `v_t ≠ Ã_Q(pt_t ‖ col_g)`, the left side is a non-zero polynomial of degree at most `s_g` in the batching challenges (degree 1 if they are independent, `s_g − 1` if they are powers of one challenge), so it vanishes with probability at most `s_max/q'` (Schwartz–Zippel). A union over the `G` groups and the `L = |List_γ(U)|` matrices gives `G · L · s_max/q'`. Outside that event every claim of every group equals `Ã*(pt_t ‖ col_g)`: all claims are consistent with the one matrix `Q*`.

**The list size.** `L ≤ μ = johnsonMu(n, D, η)`. Take any `r` distinct matrices `Q_1, …, Q_r` in `List_γ(U)`. For each pair the set of `z` with `z · (Q_a − Q_b) = 0` is a proper subspace of `Fp2^M` (the kernel of a non-zero linear map), of density at most `1/q'`, so for `r² < q'` some `z'` separates every pair, and `z' · Q_1, …, z' · Q_r` are `r` distinct codewords each agreeing with `z' · U` on at least `(1 − γ) n ≥ A_thr` coordinates (on the agreement set of its matrix). The Johnson interpolation certificate of [ArkLib26] `Johnson/Agreement.lean` is "a nonzero ordinary equation whose roots include every candidate polynomial above the agreement threshold", of degree `μ` in the candidate coordinate (`johnsonMu`, "the degree bound in the candidate-polynomial coordinate"), so `r ≤ μ`. This is the Guruswami–Sudan list bound; the reviewer should confirm from the Lean source that the certificate's degree in the candidate coordinate is `μ` (the author read the module documentation and the definition, not the proof).

**Step 5, the rows are over `Fp`.** Each `Q*_row` is an `Fp2`-polynomial of degree below `k'` that agrees with the `Fp`-valued `U_row` on `A`, and `|A| ≥ (1 − γ) n ≥ k'` points of `D ⊆ Fp`. Let `σ` be the Frobenius automorphism of `Fp2` over `Fp` applied to coefficients; for `x ∈ Fp`, `σ(Q*_row)(x) = σ(Q*_row(x))`, which equals `Q*_row(x)` on `A` because those values lie in `Fp`. Two polynomials of degree below `k'` agreeing on at least `k'` points are equal, so `σ(Q*_row) = Q*_row` and every coefficient lies in `Fp`; the messages (values on `H ⊆ Fp`) are `Fp` values, as the gadgets and lookups of RFC-0006 and RFC-0009 require.

**Step 6, what does not change.** Hiding (RFC-0013 'Hiding openings'): the mask argument needs the opened distinct columns to number at most `P_A` (`P_W`); 130 ≤ 160 and 68 ≤ 2^16 hold with more room than today, the combined rows still carry `M_ξ` and `M_g`, and the coefficient stream is public, so the simulator is unchanged. Blinding rows (RFC-0013) are committed rows of `U` like any other and need no separate treatment in steps 2 to 5. Grinding (RFC-0014 'Grinding'): in non-interactive mode `ξ` and the `γ_t` are squeezed from the transcript after the seed, so a retry costs the 20-bit `seed-pow`; `ε_MCA` and the small terms are "bounds an adversary could otherwise retry" and receive the same 20 bits the proximity row receives today, which is why EXP-0003 asked for 80 one-shot bits. In live mode the coefficients follow from the coin-tossed seed and are one-shot.

**Error budget.** Accepting a false opening has probability at most

`1/q'` (step 1) `+ ε_MCA` (step 2) `+ (1 − γ)^τ` (case a) `+ (ρ')^τ` (step 3) `+ G · L · s_max/q'` (step 4),

plus the hash-collision row counted elsewhere. Today's row is `((1 + ρ')/2)^τ + n/|Fp2|`; the new row is `(1 − γ)^τ + ε_MCA` plus terms below 2^-100 (table below). The weight commitment (RFC-0012, label `pcs/weights/{T}/xi`) has the same message shape and the same argument with `τ_W`, `P_W` and its own rows.

## Analysis

The argument closes without any new protocol message and without a property beyond what [ArkLib26] states for affine spaces: the `ξ` check with uniform coefficients does the binding (step 2 fixes the agreement set `A` and the matrix `Q*`), and the group checks are then forced through `A` alone (step 3), which is why the group coefficients `c_g` need no proximity property of their own and why the eq-structured `c_g` of RFC-0011 step 2 can stay as it is. The two places where the Johnson regime differs from unique decoding are exactly the two places the criterion named: the commitment binds to a list, not to one message, so the specification's "exactly one passing witness" (SP-3 as RFC-0011's soundness paragraph states it) must be restated as "every accepted proof is consistent with exactly one decoded matrix", after which Lemma 0 of RFC-0009 and the range analysis of RFC-0006 apply to that matrix's messages; and the prover's late choice among the list costs a union bound of `G · L · s_max/q'`, negligible because the Guruswami–Sudan list is a few hundred codewords.

Threats to validity: the exact-agreement decomposition is read from the Lean statement, whose hypotheses were checked term by term above, but the author did not step through the proof; the list bound `L ≤ μ` is read from the module documentation and the definition of `johnsonMu` and should be confirmed in the Lean source by the reviewer; RFC-0013's masked evaluation check (`u'_g`, `q_g` in `Round2Response`) was not re-read here, and the RFC must check that the masking of `u_g` composes with step 4 (the mask is a public linear shift of the message, so it should); the batching-challenge structure (independent or powers) changes `s_max` by at most a factor `s_max`, inside the margin; the weight commitment's blinding rows and segments were taken to be rows of the same matrix, as RFC-0012 describes them.

## Second reading (2026-10-16)

An independent reviewer, briefed to break the argument as a malicious prover and given the Lean files, read it the same day; the report is in [review.md](review.md) verbatim. Outcome against the pre-registered criterion: **`inconclusive`** for the protocol RFC-0021 targets, because the argument closes only after two message reorderings, which is the criterion's third clause; **holds with corrections** for the live, non-hiding protocol the argument models (RFC-0011 steps 4 to 8).

What the reviewer found, in order of weight:

1. **The hiding protocol's group check binds nothing (review item 4).** RFC-0013 'Hiding openings' adds the blinding claim `q_g = ⟨Q_g, eq(col_g)⟩` to the group check with coefficient 1, and RFC-0014 sends `q_g` inside `advice_combined`, absorbed at transcript order 8 after `pcs/advice/gamma` is squeezed at order 7. Step 3 binds `u'_g` to the committed rows, but nothing binds `q_g`: a prover with arbitrary false `v_t` sets `q_g := ⟨u'_g, eq(col_g)⟩ − Σ_t γ_t v_t` after seeing `γ` and passes. Step 6's sentence "blinding rows need no separate treatment" was wrong. The weight opening of RFC-0012 has the same shape with no batching challenge at all, so `(v_T + Δ, q_T − Δ)` passes whatever the absorb order. Both defects pre-date this experiment and RFC-0021; they are the ledger's first queue item (issues for the maintainer) and the revised Draft RFC-0021 fixes them: every blinding claim is absorbed before the batching challenges and carries its own squeezed coefficient.
2. **Grinding does not cover `ε_MCA` in non-interactive mode as drafted (review item 5).** `pcs/advice/xi` is squeezed at order 7, after the stages' final evaluations, which have slack (several values, one check), so a prover re-rolls `ξ` without redoing `seed-pow`; the `ni-100` field term is 80.4 one-shot bits, not 100.4. Step 6's grinding sentence was wrong. The revised Draft squeezes `ξ` immediately after the seed, where a retry costs the seed's proof of work; the exceptional set depends on `U` only, so an early `ξ` costs the argument nothing.
3. **The list bound's citation (review item 2).** `johnsonMu` is not stated as a list bound at the pinned commit; the conclusion follows from the certificate's fields (`jetDegree_le`, `specialization_sound`) by a derivation, not a cited statement, so the criterion's "every step on a cited primary statement" was not met as written. The matrix-to-word reduction of "The list size" is confirmed.
4. **Wording and conventions (review items 1, 6).** The `LineExactAgreementBound` glue is the `have hline` inside `mcaError_affineLine_johnson_le` (`Johnson/Probability.lean:50-62`); the hypothesis `k' ≤ n(1 − γ)` holds by a margin of at least 1.07×10^6 points at every set, not by the inequality written; `Q* = P₀` and `w_ξ = ξ_1 · P` (the scaling applies to the sent row, not to `Q*`); the extracted `U` should be defined from the Merkle leaf preimages as a step 0; the shape check of RFC-0011 step 8.1 is what bounds `deg M_ξ`; one convention `ρ₋ = (k + P − 1)/n` should serve `γ`, `b_J`, `A_thr` and `E₀`, with integer-square-root ceilings in the calculators and `A_thr` pinned, since the two conventions differ by one in `A_thr` at some sets; the budget is per commitment and a proof has eleven.

## Second reading of the revised argument (2026-10-16)

A second independent reader took the revised Draft RFC-0021 (steps 0 to 5, the schedule, the budget, the parameter sets) with the Lean files, issues #237 and #103 and the specification; the report is in [review2.md](review2.md) verbatim. Verdict: **the revised soundness argument holds as written**, every step on a cited Lean statement whose hypotheses were matched one for one (`LineToAffine.lean:193-207`, `Johnson/Probability.lean:50-62`, `Johnson/Agreement.lean:104-117`, `FiniteBounds.lean:66-108`, `PairwiseJohnson.lean:75-81, 164-168`, `ProximityGenerator/Basic.lean:341-342`), the parameter table exact and reproduced by both tools, `A_thr` identical under the integer and floating computations at every set and at 600 swept slacks. The three defects of the first reading are confirmed repaired. What the second reader found:

1. **A zero-knowledge defect in RFC-0013 and RFC-0012, pre-existing and not covered by #237.** Blinding rows are committed `Fp` rows while combined rows are `Fp2` vectors, so one blinding row masks only the `Fp`-span: with coefficient 1, `Im(u'_g) = Σ_row Im(c_g(row)) · A_row` is revealed in the clear, and the proximity row reveals one uniformly random `Fp`-linear combination of every advice row per proof. RFC-0013's "`u'_g` is uniform because `Q_g` is used exactly once" and its simulator step 4 are false for this reason, and the first Draft RFC-0021 repeated the claim. The repair in the revised Draft: two `Fp` blinding rows per `Fp2` combined row, combined as one virtual `Fp2` row `B_g = Q_g^(0) + i · Q_g^(1)`, which is uniform on `Fp2^k` exactly; one blinding claim and one coefficient per combined row as before. Reported to the maintainer for an issue; the same root cause is flagged, unverified, for the masked sumcheck's `Fp` mask coefficients and the LogUp leaf `δ`.
2. **Wording that was wrong.** "One-shot" does not describe the 7b batching coefficients: they follow prover messages with slack and are re-rollable in both modes at one or more hashes per attempt, so they are budgeted as `λ = −log2(term)` in RFC-0014's `2^{t−λ}` sense (118 bits at the pinned shape), not credited with grinding and not called one-shot; in live mode only `ξ` and the queries are one-shot per session. The coefficient shape had to be pinned (independent per claim, term `G · L/|Fp2|`, 2^-118.4 at `L = 49`; the powers shape would carry `(s + 1)`); the first `terms.txt` reported the powers-shape number under the independent-shape label.
