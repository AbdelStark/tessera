## 1. Early `ξ` (order 3b): HOLDS

- The exceptional set is `Finset.univ.filter fun x ↦ IsMCA (AffineSpaceGenerator F s) (code domain k) x U radius` (`LineToAffine.lean:207`): a function of `U`, the radius and the code only. Nothing the prover sends after 3b enters it.
- Every row of `U` is fixed before the seed: advice root in `Round1` (RFC-0014:52, absorbed at order 2 before `seed` at order 3); encoding masks drawn from the per-proof PRG during the witness pass (RFC-0011:72; INV-ZK-3, RFC-0013:122, "never from the transcript"); blinding rows `Q_g` are committed advice rows (RFC-0013:81); masked-sumcheck polynomials `G_k` committed in Round 1 (RFC-0013:52); mask rows `R_V` are advice rows (RFC-0013:67); weight blinding rows are committed per epoch and reserved before `Round1` (RFC-0012:44, 79). RFC-0010's stage messages are round polynomials and evaluations (RFC-0010:110-111), never committed data. So nothing lets the prover adapt rows or masks to `ξ`.
- Knowing `ξ` before the stages lets the prover know `A = Agr(w_ξ, ξ·U)` when it chooses its claims and `c_g`. Step 2 needs no property of `c_g` (public, adversarial is fine) and step 3 is a probability over the 7b coefficients, squeezed after every claim (orders 5, 6, 7a). The queries (order 10) are independent of `A` in both modes. No new attack.
- Retry cost in NI mode: `ξ` depends on the state after `seed`; any change before it (new `Round1`, even only a new `session_id`) needs a new valid `seed-pow` nonce for the new state, about 2^20 hashes (RFC-0004:111 accepts any nonce that meets the bits, so the next valid nonce also costs 2^20 expected). The 20-bit credit is correct.
- Two completeness points, not attacks: (a) RFC-0021 never says which row carries coefficient 1 (where `Q_ξ` sits in the layout); both sides must derive it from public data (INV-PCS-1), so the layout rule must be written. (b) In v0.3 there is no blinding row (RFC-0012:130, RFC-0013:140), so the generator is fully linear and EXP-0005 step 1's `1/|Fp2|` scaling term (`ξ_1 = 0`) is missing from RFC-0021's budget row. Negligible (2^-128), but the row claims exactness; either add the term or fix coefficient 1 on the first non-zero row in v0.3 as well, which matches `AffineSpaceGenerator = Fin.cons 1 x` (`ProximityGenerator/Basic.lean:341-342`) with no scaling step.

## 2. Blinding claims at 7a/7b: binding HOLDS; the hiding claim is WRONG (pre-existing RFC-0013 defect, restated); two consistency gaps

Binding (step 3). With step 2 giving `u'_g = c_g·Q*` as polynomials where `c_g = Σ_t γ_{g,t} eq(pt_t,·) + γ_{g,Q} e_Q` is public after 7b, the group check reads `Σ_t γ_{g,t}(v_t − Ã*(pt_t‖col_g)) + γ_{g,Q}(q_g − Q̃*_g(col_g)) = 0`. With `v_t` absorbed at orders 5/6 and `q_g` at 7a, a wrong claim for a fixed `Q` makes this a non-zero linear form in independent uniform `Fp2` variables: zero with probability exactly `1/|Fp2|`. The prover picks `Q*` after 7b (through `w_ξ` at order 8), so the union over `List_{A_thr}(U)` is needed and `|List| ≤ L` by the separating-functional reduction of EXP 'The list size' plus `closePolynomialSet_finite_and_ncard_le_pairwiseJohnson` (`PairwiseJohnson.lean:75-81`; the bound unfolds to `n(A−D)/(A·A−nD)` in ℕ, lines 164-168). `G·L/|Fp2|` is right. Weights: `u_T = c_T·Q*_T` with `c_T = eq(ρ_ℓ,ℓ)·eq(r_o^hi,o_hi)` on data rows and `γ_T` on `β_T`'s row, so the check is `(v_T − W̃*_T) + γ_T(q_T − β̃*_T) = 0`; `v_T` (order 6) and `q_T` (7a) precede `γ_T` (7b); a wrong `v_T` survives for at most one `γ_T`. Binds. Consistent with #237's second admissible shape (fresh challenge scaling the blinding row; for weights exactly `u_T = fold + θ_T β_T`, check `v_T + θ_T q_T`).

Gap 2a: the RFC is internally inconsistent on the shape. RFC-0021:51 "adopts the shape #237 lands", RFC-0021:56 and INV-PCS-6 assert "powers of one challenge are not used". If #237 lands its first shape (claims at `γ^{t+1}`, blinding at `γ^0`), the per-check term becomes `(s_g+1)/|Fp2|` and the row `G·L·(s_max+1)/|Fp2|`, with `s_max` from the claim list. Related: RFC-0021:92 cites `results/terms.txt` for "below 2^-109" but that file computes `G·L·claims_max/q'` with `claims_max = 2^8` (terms.py:23, 41) = 2^-109.8, which is the powers-shape term; the RFC's own formula `G·L/|Fp2|` gives 2^-117.8 (G = 16, L = 73). Pin one shape and make the formula, terms.py and the cited number agree.

Gap 2b: cross-reference: RFC-0021:56 "(the argument's step 4)" should be step 3 (step 4 is Frobenius).

Gap 2c: the weight formula assumes one claim per matrix. RFC-0010:128 sends channel-parameter claims to the weight openings; if `ChannelParams` carries more than one claim, `v_T + γ_T q_T` must become `Σ_t γ_{T,t} v_t + γ_{T,Q} q_T`. Verify the claim count; the RFC's general rule covers it but the written check does not.

WRONG: hiding. RFC-0021:55 says `γ_{g,Q}·Q_g` and `γ_T·β_T` are uniform whenever the coefficient is non-zero. Committed rows are `Fp`-valued: `advice_columns: Vec<Vec<Fp>>` (RFC-0014:64), `symbols: [Fp; ≤ B]` (RFC-0004:39), `push_row(.., values: &[Fp])` (RFC-0011:64); blinding rows are "random message rows" of the matrix (RFC-0012:44, RFC-0013:81, glossary), and step 4 of the argument itself relies on `U` being `Fp`-valued. Combined rows are `Fp2^k` (RFC-0011:81-82). So `u'_g = c_g·A + γ_{g,Q} Q_g` with `Q_g ∈ Fp^k`: the mask ranges over the `Fp`-subspace `γ_{g,Q}·Fp^k` of `Fp`-dimension `k` inside `Fp2^k` of `Fp`-dimension `2k`; the image of `u'_g` in `Fp2^k/(γ_{g,Q}Fp^k) ≅ Fp^k` equals the image of `c_g·A`, coordinate-wise `(b·Re − a·Im)((c_g·A)_x)` for `γ_{g,Q} = a + bi`: `k` witness-determined `Fp` values per combined row in the clear. With coefficient 1 (RFC-0013:81 as accepted) it is simply `Im(u'_g) = Σ_row Im(c_g(row))·A_row`. For the proximity row, `Im(u_ξ) = Σ_row Im(ξ_row)·A_row`: one uniformly random `Fp`-linear combination of every advice row revealed per proof (the same for `eq(ξ*,row)` today). RFC-0013:81 "`u'_g` is uniform because `Q_g` is used exactly once" and RFC-0013:102 (simulator step 4) are false for the same reason; the simulator samples from a `(2k−2)`-dimensional affine space while the real `u'_g` lies in a `(k−2)`-dimensional one, so a distinguisher succeeds with probability about 1 and SP-2 is not met. This is not a soundness bug and does not touch steps 0–5 (more rows of `U` change nothing), but RFC-0021 repeats the false claim, and #237 (binding only) does not fix it. Fix: two `Fp` blinding rows per `Fp2` combined row, each with its own coefficient; proximity row: one with coefficient 1 and one with a uniform `ξ` (still exactly the affine generator); group rows: `γ_{g,Q0}`, `γ_{g,Q1}` independent, two blinding claims (the linear-form argument is unchanged). `u'_g` is then uniform on `Fp2^k` unless the two coefficients are `Fp`-dependent (about 2^-64). Cost: 34 advice rows instead of 17; weights 5 blinding rows per matrix per proof (evaluation 2, proximity 2, mask row 1; the scalar `R̃_V(p_col)` is uniform in `Fp2` from one `Fp` row because the `Fp`-span of `eq(p_col,·)` is `Fp2`), so about 409 proofs per epoch at `B_W = 2048` (RFC-0012:84) unless `B_W` grows. Same root cause, outside RFC-0021 but worth its own issue: the masked sumcheck's `g_j` have `Fp` coefficients (`n(d+1)+1` committed elements, RFC-0013:52-57) while round messages are `Fp2[X]`; the mask adds `2^{n−j}ρ_k·g_j(X)` whose coefficients lie on the `Fp`-line `ρ_k·Fp`, so one `Fp`-component of each of the `d` non-constant coefficients of every round message is unmasked. The LogUp leaf `(0, δ)` with `δ ∈ Fp` multiplies `d_root` by an element of `Fp*`, leaving `d_root`'s class in `Fp2*/Fp*` (about 64 bits of `Π(α−κ)`) exposed, if `δ` is committed as an `Fp` advice value as written (RFC-0013:86). Verify both; I did not read RFC-0008 for the leaf's exact denominator.

## 3. Live mode: HOLDS with a wording correction

Session order (RFC-0014:31-41): `ξ` follows the seed; the seed is uniform if either party is honest (INV-PROTO-2) and one per `Round1` (INV-PROTO-1), so a retry is a fresh commitment (about 2^41 hash compressions, a visible abort): one-shot is right for `ε_MCA`. Queries are the verifier's after `Round2Response` is absorbed (INV-PROTO-3): one-shot for `(1−γ)^τ` and `(D/n)^τ`. Wrong as written: RFC-0021:80 "In live mode every challenge follows from the coin-tossed seed once per session (INV-PROTO-1)". `Round2Response` is one message computed offline; its Fiat–Shamir challenges (every `stage/{k}/r/{j}` and the 7b coefficients) are re-rollable by varying a stage's final evaluations within its check (RFC-0010:111-112) before sending, in live mode exactly as in NI mode; INV-PROTO-1 constrains only the seed. The budget conclusion is unchanged: the 7b term is 2^-109.8 (or 2^-117.8) per attempt at one or more hashes per attempt, so `λ ≥ 109.8` in RFC-0014's `2^{t−λ}` sense (RFC-0014:92), above both targets. Malicious verifier: `ξ` is a public deterministic function of the seed, no earlier or later than the stage challenges it already learned; `q_g` at 7a is determined by `u'_g`, the claims and the coefficients (when `γ_{g,Q} ≠ 0`) and arrives in the same message as before. Nothing new is learned (the hiding defect of item 2 is independent of the order).

## Overall verdict

Holds with these corrections: the revised soundness argument (steps 0–5) is correct for the protocol as revised and every step rests on the cited Lean statements with hypotheses met; the parameter table is exact and reproduced. The corrections are: (1) the 'Hiding' bullet is false; a single `Fp` blinding row cannot make an `Fp2` combined row uniform (RFC-0013 defect, repeated; not soundness, not covered by #237; needs its own issue and two blinding rows per combination); (2) pin the coefficient shape and make the budget formula, terms.py and the cited 2^-109 agree (G·L/|Fp2| = 2^-117.8 with independent coefficients, ×(s_max+1) with powers); (3) "one-shot" is wrong for the 7b coefficients in both modes (re-rollable; λ ≥ 109.8 in the 2^{t−λ} sense, conclusion unchanged); (4) add the v0.3 `1/|Fp2|` term or fix coefficient 1 in v0.3, and specify which row carries coefficient 1; (5) the cross-references and the OQ-0021-2 wording.
