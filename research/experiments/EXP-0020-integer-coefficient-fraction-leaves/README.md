## Hypothesis

The fixed initial integer-coefficient window depth **v=2**, applied to the bottom lookup and table fraction levels of every judged resident case below, saves at least 5% of complete LogUp proof time against both current production and a current-relation EXP-0013-style deferred-product comparator, and at least 30% of complete bottom-level time against production, while reproducing every message, claim and transcript state with independent acceptance.

## Success criterion

Only v=2 is timed and judged. Depths 1 and 3 are correctness/bound controls; there is no post-hoc window selection. For case `c`, arm `X` and paired block `i`, let `W_Xci` be elapsed time per complete proof, including creation/destruction of every per-proof buffer, and `L_Xci` the separate complete bottom-batch level invocation described below. Set `sP_cv = 1-median_i(W_Cvci/W_Pci)`, `sD_cv = 1-median_i(W_Cvci/W_Dci)` and `lP_cv = 1-median_i(L_Cvci/L_Pci)`. Also compute these three savings using aggregate elapsed time divided by aggregate repetitions for each arm. Let `G_2` be the minimum of both whole-proof savings, paired and aggregate, over all six judged cases; let `H_2` be the minimum of the paired and aggregate bottom-level savings over those cases. No geometric mean or favorable subset substitutes for these minima.

- **`validated`:** `G_2 >= 0.05` and `H_2 >= 0.30`; every exact check and required arm/ratio/sham noise gate passes. Each profile must pass at both lookup heights; no per-profile depth or threshold changes.
- **`refuted`:** all exact checks and required noise gates pass, and `G_2 < 0.02` or `H_2 < 0.15`. This refutes this bounded resident implementation/criterion, not every possible small-value prover.
- **`inconclusive`:** everything else, including the numerical gaps, unresolved noise, an inability to implement the registered algebra/resource limits, or correctness blockage. A wrong digest is a harness defect, not a speedup or mechanism refutation. Fix it under the frozen method or report the blockage; changes to domains, windows, arithmetic or comparator require a dated reviewed amendment committed before dependent code.

Production and deferred comparisons are both primary. C uses D's same continuation above the leaves and after its window; D is therefore the incremental comparator that prevents already validated deferred arithmetic being credited to integer coefficients. EXP-0012's product-window refutation and fraction-grid inconclusive result remain unchanged. There is no Stwo dependency, fitted GPU unit cost, new cost-model option or whole-model rescore.

### Exact coefficient basis and window algorithm

Let original even/odd leaf halves at a grid point be `(kL,vL,mL)` and `(kR,vR,mR)`, where m is nonnegative before extrapolation and table numerators are `-m`. Set `dL=alpha-kL-beta*vL` and similarly dR. The fixed denominator-product basis/order is:

```text
S0=1; Sk=kL+kR; Sv=vL+vR;
Skk=kL*kR; Skv=kL*vR+vL*kR; Svv=vL*vR.
dL*dR = alpha^2*S0 - alpha*Sk - alpha*beta*Sv
        + Skk + beta*Skv + beta^2*Svv.
lookup numerator cross = 2*alpha*S0 - Sk - beta*Sv.
```

For the table side append, in this fixed order, `Sm=mL+mR`, `Smk=mL*kR+mR*kL`, `Smv=mL*vR+mR*vL`. Its numerator cross is `-alpha*Sm + Smk + beta*Smv`. The required relation is numerator cross plus `lambda*dL*dR`; lambda is the actual within-instance challenge, and gamma/current scale/front-padding factors remain the outer field factors from production. Do not substitute EXP-0013's proxy gamma polynomial. All identities are over integers reduced modulo P, not a claim that raw integer sums remain below P.

For primary v=2 (and correctness-only v=1,3), form multilinear extrapolations of each raw column on `{0,1,2}^v`, using `x(2)=2*x(1)-x(0)` in signed integer arithmetic. Each grid cell sums its coefficient channels over the Boolean remaining coordinates, weighted by their split-eq `w_lo` components, reducing each public bucket as below; multiply by `w_hi` after reduction and add in Fp2. S0's weighted sum is exactly 1 because remaining eq weights sum to 1; compute this constant analytically, not by a hidden per-cell pass. For R, omit the publicly identically zero Sv/Skv/Svv/Smv channels; no zero skipping based on witness values is allowed. Thus E/Q use five nonconstant product channels on lookup and eight on table; R uses two and four respectively. Combine reduced coefficients with the displayed challenge monomials in Fp2, including every multiplication/addition in E0 counts and E1 timing.

The resulting grid Q is degree at most two in each window coordinate. Before sending round j, contract its later still-Boolean window coordinates with the actual `eq(point_j,bit)` weights; multiply the current coordinate's linear eq factor and the previous eq/current-instance scale to obtain the same degree-three round polynomial. Evaluate at the current production nodes and preserve its claim recovery/degenerate fallback. After the challenge, collapse that grid axis with quadratic Lagrange interpolation; include every collapse and contraction. After all v challenges, bind the original even/odd columns at those challenges once, set denominators to `alpha-k_bound-beta*v_bound` and numerators to 1 or `-m_bound`, and continue D. Do not treat the original bounded integers as still bounded after field challenges. This is coefficient evaluation, not EXP-0012's field-denominator grid.

### Exact signed bound, reduction cadence and public fallback

The fixed v=2 choice limits the grid to 9 cells (rather than 27 at v3) while deferring the first two binds; it tests the smallest nontrivial multi-round coefficient window. This is an E0 cost-scope choice, not a claim that v2 is globally optimal. For public K,V,M bounds and `A=3^v`, extrapolated coordinates have absolute bounds AK, AV, AM. Every coefficient channel has absolute value at most

```text
Tmax=max(1,2*A*K,2*A*V,A^2*K^2,2*A^2*K*V,A^2*V^2,
         2*A*M,2*A^2*M*K,2*A^2*M*V).
```

Terms involving m are absent on lookup but included in the common conservative bound. A weighted accumulator component receives a canonical split-eq limb `0<=w<=P-1` times a signed coefficient. Pin `B=1024`: reduce and reset every bucket of at most 1024 original remaining-coordinate tuples, including a final shorter bucket; split a larger eq_lo group, never extend cadence based on observed values. Use two's-complement **signed 192-bit** sums independently for both Fp2 components of each active channel. A signed coefficient is held in i128, its magnitude multiplied by a u64 weight with two `64x64->128` partial products, carry-combined into three words, then sign-applied by fixed-word masks/negation; no data-dependent limb count, division, branch or indexing. The zero/negative/carry cases are mandatory controls. Reduction uses fixed three-word Goldilocks reduction; a negative two's-complement value needs subtraction of `2^192 mod P = 1`, determined by a sign mask. Check this independently against Python signed integers modulo P; ordinary unsigned EXP-0013 reduction alone is insufficient.

Dispatch to this path only if the **public** envelope proves `A*max(K,V,M)<=2^63-1`, `Tmax<2^127` and `B*(P-1)*Tmax<2^191`. Use checked arithmetic for public bound evaluation; overflow means fallback before forming a coefficient. All judged cases have `K<=2^22-1`, `V<=65536`, `M<=2^22`, v<=3. Their common worst-case v3 Tmax is `25,649,401,137,463,296`, with exact weighted bucket bound `484503488836133630691506044003449569280 < 2^129 < 2^191`. There are no extra partial-product or cross-term additions outside the listed channel bounds; E0 checks every channel separately as well. The original 64/128-bit universal claim is not used.

The general-field fallback is **D's ordinary materialized-leaf path for the entire instance**, selected by public domain metadata, mode and shape before reading values, never by whether a secret coefficient happens to fit. Unsupported extension numerator/denominator inputs or missing trustworthy integer metadata also select D. Mask corrections are outside the unmasked judged path: mask-like field controls select D on their explicitly supplied complete field tables; no missing masked-protocol correction is invented or silently dropped. Rejected integer metadata does not reject a proof input that D supports. A dishonest out-of-envelope synthetic column is a generator/test failure; do not silently truncate or dynamically switch based on its value. Domain promises come from the experiment's exact generator, not a new verifier assumption. No production range check is removed or weakened.

D uses current split-eq groups, subdivided to at most B pairs, and the existing three-word schoolbook accumulator: for each node weight w precompute `wl=lambda*w` per public low-weight entry, then accumulate `(w*nL+wl*dL)*dR + (w*nR)*dL`; specialize n=1 and base n in the first round. Reduced scaled factors are canonical Fp2. Each component accumulator receives at most `4*B` canonical 64-bit partial products, hence is below `4*B*(P-1)^2 < 2^140 < 2^192`. Include low-weight scaling, table-base specialization and reduction overhead in the count. This preserves the exact current fraction relation while deferring only terminal products. No other ordering or tuned dispatch is selected after timing.

## Analysis
