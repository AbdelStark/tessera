## Mechanism

Setting. A batch of `B = 16,384` tokens is split by RFC-0018's planner into `U` proof units (sets of requests at short context, layer ranges of one request at long context; RFC-0018:90). Units share the seed and the stage structure, so `α, β` (squeezed right after the seed, [RFC-0014 'Transcript schedule'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0014-interaction-and-soundness.md#transcript-schedule) row 4, RFC-0014:80) are the same in every unit; but each unit has its own advice commitment, and the multiplicity polynomial of every table is committed advice (RFC-0008:48), so today each unit `u` runs, for every table definition `T`, its own combined tree with the leaves `(1, α − a_i)` for its lookups, `(−m_{u,j}, α − t_j)` for all `2^b` rows with its own multiplicities `m_{u,j}`, and the `(0, δ_u)` masking leaf ([RFC-0013 'LogUp and product-tree roots'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0013-zero-knowledge.md#logup-and-product-tree-roots), RFC-0013:86). The table side (`2^b` committed values, `2^b` leaves, the level sumchecks over them) is paid `U` times per batch.

What changes, per table definition `T` of the batch:

1. **One multiplicity column per table per batch.** P1 counts multiplicities over all units' lookups: the units partition the batch's tokens, so `m_j = Σ_u m_{u,j}` is exactly the sum of today's per-unit counts, and the counter memory of 08 §4 (`≤ 2^32 × 4 B` per table) is allocated once instead of `U` times. The columns `m` of all tables (`Σ_T 2^{b_T}` elements; `10 × 2^32 ≈ 4.3×10^10` at ten wide tables, E0) go into one **batch-level advice commitment** with the RFC-0011 layout (vanishing-polynomial masks, chained column digests, one root), opened once at the final point of the table trees. The claim list gains one field naming this commitment and its row count (a public function of the table set, so input-independent).
2. **One table-side tree per table per batch.** The leaves `(−m_j, α − t_j)`, `j < 2^{b_T}`, form one fraction tree `𝒯_T` (the "table tree") built once per batch; its leaf claims land on the batch-level multiplicity commitment and on the structured `t̃(ρ)` the verifier evaluates itself ([RFC-0008 'Structured evaluation'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0008-logup-and-tables.md#structured-evaluation)). Each unit keeps a **lookup tree** `ℒ_{u,T}` of its own lookups `(1, α − a_i)` plus its `(0, δ_u)` masking leaf plus one **shift leaf** `(r_{u,T}, 1)` (item 3b); its leaf claims land in the unit (virtual key to the producing stage, value column in the unit's advice) exactly as today.
3. **The join.** A top tree of `U + 1` leaves, the `U` roots `(n_{u,T}, d_{u,T})` of the lookup trees and the root `(n_T, d_T)` of the table tree, padded to `2^{⌈log2(U+1)⌉}` with the neutral leaf `(0, 1)`, combined by the fraction rule `(n, d) = (n_L d_R + n_R d_L, d_L d_R)` to one root with the public claim `n = 0`, `d ≠ 0`. In rationals the root says
   ```
   Σ_u ( Σ_{i ∈ u} 1/(α − a_i) + r_{u,T} )  −  Σ_j m_j/(α − t_j)  +  r_{T}  =  0,
   ```
   which is RFC-0008's relation over the batch's lookups once `Σ_u r_{u,T} + r_T = 0` (3b). The `⌈log2(U + 1)⌉` join levels are ordinary degree-3 fraction-tree levels ([RFC-0008 'Fraction tree'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0008-logup-and-tables.md#fraction-tree), RFC-0008:47; [RFC-0010 'Tree levels'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0010-proof-schedule.md#tree-levels)), batched with the other trees' levels of the leaf stage exactly as trees of different heights join today ("at the level where their height matches", RFC-0008:53); the unit trees and the table tree then continue level by level as today, from the claims the join levels hand them.
   - **3a. Nothing per-unit is public.** Only the joined root is `n = 0`. The verifier never sees a unit's root in the clear; it sees what every level reveals (the children's evaluations at the level's random point, OQ-0013-2).
   - **3b. Shift leaves.** Because a level reveals its children at points with one Boolean coordinate (OQ-0013-2, RFC-0013:154) and at `U = 1` the top level's children *are* the unit root and the table root, the unit root's ratio `n_{u,T}/d_{u,T} = Σ_{i ∈ u} 1/(α − a_i)` would be revealed (a function of the secret lookups). Each lookup tree therefore carries one shift leaf `(r_{u,T}, 1)` and the table tree one shift leaf `(r_T, 1)`, with `r_{u,T}` uniform in `Fp2` and `r_T = −Σ_u r_{u,T}`; the `U + 1` values are committed in the batch-level advice commitment (a column of `U + 1` entries per table) and the verifier checks the linear claim `Σ_u r_{u,T} + r_T = 0` through the opening (a sum over Boolean points of a committed column is a multilinear claim like any other, [RFC-0010 'Claim pool and openings'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0010-proof-schedule.md#claim-pool-and-openings)). A denominator of `1` can never coincide with a table row's `α − t_j` except when `α = t_j + 1` (probability `T/|Fp2|`, inside the LogUp error), so a shift leaf cannot stand in for a table row; this is the difference from the rejected "mirrored mask" variant (see Security gate).
4. **Claims.** The table tree's leaf claims: `m̃(ρ_T)` on the batch-level commitment, `t̃(ρ_T)` evaluated by the verifier, once per table per batch. The unit trees' leaf claims: unchanged, in their units. The batch-level commitment is opened once, in the same `ColumnQueries` round as the units' commitments (RFC-0014 rows 4–5 of the session order), with its own `pcs/advice/...` labels suffixed by the batch scope.
5. **Transcript.** The join levels are the first `⌈log2(U + 1)⌉` levels of the leaf stage, under the `tree/{ℓ}/...` labels of RFC-0014 row 5 at the batch scope, before the units' levels; the batch already carries cross-unit material (the boundary-residual equality claims of RFC-0018:90), so a batch scope exists. `U`, the table set and the tree heights are planner outputs from public sizes (the batch manifest), and the verifier derives them from the manifest, never from the prover.
6. **Degenerate case.** At `U = 1` the construction reduces to today's combined tree plus two shift leaves and one extra level; the planner may skip the join when `U = 1` and the RFC says so, so the short-context proof is unchanged.

Saving per batch: `(U − 1) × Σ_T 2^{b_T}` committed elements and as many table leaves (with their share of the level sumchecks), and `(U − 1)` fewer multiplicity-counter arrays. Cost per batch: one small commitment (`Σ_T 2^{b_T} + Σ_T (U + 1)` elements), `(U + 1)` leaves and `⌈log2(U + 1)⌉` levels per table, one linear claim per table.

## Why it could work

- **The term is real and multiplied by `U`.** [RFC-0018](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0018-gpu-prover-runtime.md#pass-structure) (E0, primary, RFC-0018:90): units "have their own advice commitments"; [RFC-0008](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0008-logup-and-tables.md#fraction-tree) (E0, primary, RFC-0008:44, 48): multiplicities are committed advice and table rows are leaves of the same tree as the lookups; so on today's reading each unit pays `Σ_T 2^{b_T}` committed multiplicities and as many table leaves. [08 §4](../../docs/spec/08-performance-budget.md#4-memory-budgets) (E0, primary, 08:62) budgets the counters "amortized over the batch", an amortization RFC-0018 does not deliver at `U > 1`. The spec is at least ambiguous here (kill criterion 2 is the reading that makes the saving vanish).
- **Native shape of the protocol.** [PH23] (E0, primary): LogUp-GKR commits one multiplicity column per table *instance*; the instance is the natural unit of sharing. [Stwo26] (E0, primary, code read in SUR-0007): GKR instances of different heights join one batched sumcheck at matching depth, which the join relies on and RFC-0008 'Batching' already assumes. [Jo26] (E0, primary): the once-per-proof table term is one of the three levers the separation principle leaves open; this idea moves it without touching per-lookup cost.
- **Pricing (IDEA-0023's recipe, E0, primary).** One `2^32`-row table costs `2^32 × (c_c + c_ℓ) = 2^32 × 70 ps = 0.30 s` per proof unit at the `corrected` prices and `2^32 × 195 ps = 0.84 s` at the `counted` ones when its leaf is priced at `lookup_ps` (`×0.6` at `leaf_ps`; range 42–195 ps per row). Ten wide tables at `U = 8` cost `7 × 10 × 2^32 / 16,384 = 1.83×10^7` extra committed elements and leaves per token against `C_lin = 2.82×10^7` (corrected profile): the duplicated table side alone is 65% of the linear-layer count (E0; IDEA-0023:39 prices the single copy at 9%).
- **Cross-unit sharing has measured precedent.** [STS26] (E1, primary; SUR-0010 row): per-request cost falls from 99 s to 15.2 s at 12 packed requests because cost follows sealed boundaries, not tokens. [SGO+26] (E1 relative, primary, abstract only): sub-proofs over layer ranges linked by shared boundary commitments, up to 6.84× over a monolithic proof. Neither shares a table side; the nearest neighbours.
- **`U` is unknown.** OQ-0018-1 (SPEC.md:111) is open until v0.5; RFC-0018:90 says long requests split into "layer ranges of one request" and the planner "minimizes total time subject to the proof-size budget". At 405B/16K/16K the model's `c ≈ 9` streamed passes (`--json`, E0) are the pressure that makes the planner split; `U` between 2 and 8 is plausible (claim level, this note). At 1K context, units are request sets and `U = 1` is the likely default (claim level).

## Security gate

| Property | Effect | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| Soundness bits (G-4, RFC-0014) | The joined root with `n = 0` states `Σ_u Σ_{i ∈ u} 1/(α − a_i) + Σ_u r_{u,T} − Σ_j m_j/(α − t_j) + r_T = 0`; with the linear claim `Σ_u r_{u,T} + r_T = 0` checked through the opening, this is RFC-0008's relation over the batch's `N_batch = Σ_u N_u` lookups (the same tokens the units partitioned), so a lookup outside the table in any unit makes the identity fail for all but `N_batch + T + U` values of `α`: error `(N_batch + T + U + 1)/\|Fp2\|`, the RFC-0014 budget line "`N + T ≤ 2^38` per instance" (RFC-0014:99) now checked once per batch instance instead of once per unit (IDEA-0023's soundness refuter put a fully merged INT8 requant instance at `2^36.6–2^37.3` at 16K, margin 1.6–2.5×, E0; the planner must check the line per batch instance). The join levels are degree-3 fraction-tree levels with the per-level error of RFC-0010 (RFC-0010:95). `α` is squeezed after the batch-level commitment and every unit's `Round1` are absorbed (RFC-0014 rows 2–4), so the multiplicities and the shift values are fixed before `α` (INV-FS-1). The shift leaves have denominator `1`, which equals some `α − t_j` only when `α = t_j + 1` (`T/\|Fp2\|`, absorbed in the LogUp error), so a shift leaf cannot impersonate a table row; and the `r` values are opened from one commitment, so no unit can carry a private copy. Two prover-controlled degrees of freedom must be closed by the verifier, not the prover: (a) every unit's lookup tree enters the join (an omitted unit is a lookup sum left out of the LogUp identity, a defect class a public audit of Stwo's LogUp components reported in 2025, secondary, from memory; the verifier derives the unit list and `U` from the public batch manifest, RFC-0019 step 4 binds the manifest); (b) exactly one multiplicity column per table per batch (RFC-0019 accepts one opening against the batch-level root and no per-unit multiplicity claims). With both, nothing in G-4's budget moves except `N` per instance. | pass (E0) |
| Uniqueness upstream of the logits (SP-3, RFC-0009 Lemma 0) | Unchanged: the lookups, keys and tables are those of today's units; Lemma 0, 1, 2 apply per element as before. The multiplicities and shift values are LogUp advice, not witness values upstream of the logits; any `m` satisfying the identity is the true count vector when every `a_i` is a row (RFC-0008 'Relation'), as today. | pass (E0) |
| Zero knowledge (G-5, RFC-0013) | New revealed data: the join levels' per-level evaluations of their children at points with one Boolean coordinate (OQ-0013-2, RFC-0013:154), which at `U = 1` are the unit root and the table root themselves. Without 3b this reveals `n_{u,T}/d_{u,T} = Σ_{i ∈ u} 1/(α − a_i)`, a function of the unit's secret lookups: **fail**. With the shift leaves of 3b, `n_{u,T}/d_{u,T} = Σ_{i ∈ u} 1/(α − a_i) + r_{u,T}` with `r_{u,T}` uniform and committed under RFC-0011 masks, and `d_{u,T}` is multiplied by the uniform non-zero `δ_u` of the `(0, δ_u)` leaf, so each revealed unit root is a uniform pair `(n, d)` with `d ≠ 0` conditioned on the public joined root; the table root is then determined by the join and reveals the public `m`-free quantity `Σ_j m_j/(α − t_j) − r_T` shifted by the uniform `r_T`. The batch-level commitment has an input-independent shape (`Σ_T 2^{b_T} + Σ_T (U + 1)` rows, from public sizes), and `U` and the table set are planner outputs from public sizes, so the claim-list shape is input-independent and the RFC-0013 simulator extends by sampling the `r` and the roots. What has not been checked by anyone but this note: that the simulator for the join levels composes with the masked-extension argument of RFC-0013 'Masked extensions' at the Boolean coordinates OQ-0013-2 leaves open, and that counting multiplicities over the batch instead of per unit does not widen the EXP-0010 timing channel (same loop, one array instead of `U`; E0 reasoning says no). | **unknown** (pass with 3b by the E0 argument above; fail without it; the first thing the E1 prototype's simulator test settles) |
| Verifier independence (SP-6) | The verifier gains `⌈log2(U + 1)⌉` levels per table, one batch-level commitment opening, `U + 1` linear-claim checks per table, and derives `U` and the table set from the public manifest; all written from the specification in `tv-core`, no prover code involved. | pass |
| Post-quantum (hash-based commitments) | Unchanged: the batch-level commitment is an RFC-0011 hash-based commitment. | pass |

A `fail` anywhere rejects the idea. The one `unknown` (zero knowledge of the join levels with the shift leaves) is the first thing the experiment settles; it is a `fail` if the shift leaves are dropped. Design note recorded for the refuters: a variant that reveals per-unit roots and masks them with nonzero-numerator leaves whose denominators are `α − key` for keys mirrored on the table side (`(ε_u, α − k_u)` in the unit, `(−ε_u, α − k_u)` in the table tree) is unsound unless both mirrors open the same committed `(ε_u, k_u)`: with independent copies a mask pole at a bad key forges a table row. The shift leaves avoid the pole (denominator `1`) and are opened from one commitment, which is why the mechanism keeps `n = 0` public and masks the roots additively.

## Prior rejection check

Checked against [baseline.md §7](../baseline.md#7-already-rejected) and the RFCs' Alternatives Considered on 2026-10-17:

- [RFC-0008 Alternatives Considered](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0008-logup-and-tables.md#alternatives-considered) 4 (RFC-0008:122) rejects "separate lookup and table trees" because of "two roots to compare and two GKR instances where one combined tree suffices". What is new: across proof units today there are `U` combined trees and `U` table sides per table, and the join restores one root per table while removing `U − 1` table sides; the alternative's objection (two roots the verifier compares) does not arise, since the only public root is the joined one. Items 1–3, 5, 6 are on other subjects.
- [RFC-0018 Alternatives Considered](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0018-gpu-prover-runtime.md#alternatives-considered) 1–5 (RFC-0018:112-116): 2 (independent per-tile challenges) is about tile-level challenges and opening groups, not the table side; none concerns sharing across units.
- [RFC-0010 Alternatives Considered](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0010-proof-schedule.md#alternatives-considered) rejects layer-by-layer GKR and independent per-layer challenges; the join adds `⌈log2(U + 1)⌉` levels to a constant-depth leaf stage, not a per-layer structure.
- [RFC-0013 Alternatives Considered](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0013-zero-knowledge.md#alternatives-considered) (committing round messages, doubling tensors, per-layer masked evaluations): the shift leaves are `U + 1` committed values per table, not a per-layer or per-message cost.
- [IDEA-0007](IDEA-0007-dedup-table-side-wide-requant.md) (sparse table side) was rejected on zero knowledge because its public shape depended on the secret activations; here the shape is a function of public sizes (`U`, table set).
- [IDEA-0023](IDEA-0023-dilation-shared-requant-tables.md) shares tables across tensors by key dilation (fewer distinct tables); this idea shares each table across units (each table paid once per batch). Orthogonal and multiplicative: at `T` tables and `U` units today's table side is `U × T`, after both it is `5`.
- The shared-prefix idea (SUR-0010 synthesis 5, SUR-0013, ledger queue item 51) shares *committed activations* across requests and is killed by the leak of which requests share a prefix; this idea shares only the table side, whose contents are public, and leaks nothing about which tokens are in which unit beyond the public manifest.
- baseline §7 has no row on table sharing or on proof units.

New: the observation that RFC-0018's proof units multiply the once-per-proof table term by `U`, the join of per-unit lookup trees to one batch-level table tree under a single public zero root, and the additive shift-leaf masking of the join. "None" after the check.

## Kill criterion

Any one of: (1) RFC-0018's planner produces `U = 1` for the 405B/16K/16K reference point (OQ-0018-1 resolved for streaming over units), so there is nothing to share at the scored context; (2) a reading of RFC-0010 and RFC-0018 by their owner shows the leaf stage already runs each table's instance once per batch over all units' lookups with one multiplicity column (then the idea is a one-sentence clarification against RFC-0018 "Proof units" and 08 §4, not a saving); (3) the RFC-0020 gate measures the table side below 10 ps per row (zero-multiplicity blocks skipped, table leaves far cheaper than lookup leaves), making the term under 1% of `μ(16K)` even at `U = 8`.

## Cheapest experiment

Hypothesis: (a) one table tree joined to `U` lookup trees with shift leaves accepts exactly the batches whose every lookup is a row, with empirical false-accept rate within the 99% binomial interval of `(N_batch + T + U + 1)/|F|` over the test field; (b) each adversarial deviation named below is rejected with the exact `RejectReason`; (c) the join levels' revealed values are indistinguishable from uniform pairs `(n, d ≠ 0)` conditioned on the joined root (a simulator reproduces their distribution), and without shift leaves they are not.

Method, in order:
1. E0 (`s`, one day): the count table (per-unit against shared table side for `U ∈ {2, 4, 8}` and 5–50 tables at the model's two prices), the RFC-0014 `N + T` check per batch instance (merged INT8 requant instance at 16K against `2^38`), the clarification question to the RFC-0018 owner that decides kill criterion 2, and a reading of EXP-0010's bound for the batch-wide counting loop.
2. E1 (`m`, feasible here, one week): a two- and four-unit prototype over `F65537Ext2` (`tessera-field` exports the test fields; `crates/tessera-field/src/lib.rs:10, 28-29`) built like [EXP-0006](../experiments/EXP-0006-product-leaf-cost/README.md)'s fraction proxy on `tessera-sumcheck`, in its own Cargo workspace under `research/experiments/`: `U` lookup trees with `(0, δ_u)` and shift leaves, one table tree with a shared multiplicity vector and `r_T`, the join levels, verified by a driver around `tv_core`'s level check; adversarial cases, each asserting the exact rejection: a lookup in unit 2 outside the table; a multiplicity off by one; a unit tree omitted from the join (the verifier's manifest-derived `U` catches it); a second multiplicity vector supplied for unit 2; shift values not summing to zero; the mirrored-mask variant with mismatched mirrors (to demonstrate the forgery the note excludes); and the soundness calibration of [07 §4](../../docs/spec/07-testing-strategy.md) over `10^5` seeds. The zero-knowledge test: a distinguisher over the join levels' revealed pairs with and without shift leaves at `U = 1` and `U = 4`.

Reaches E1 for soundness and the zero-knowledge argument, E0 for cost. What stays unknown until v0.5 (no H100 needed for the count): `U` at the reference point (OQ-0018-1). What stays unknown until the RFC-0020 gate (E2): the per-row table-side price, 42–195 ps. Effort: `s` for the E0 step, `m` for the prototype; the superseding RFC touches tier-A table definitions and the claim list (`l` by the rubric's letter; no protocol version is frozen before v0.3).
