## Mechanism

Setting. Three paddings make a tree instance larger than its real leaf count, and all three are materialized today: `Forest::new` takes a stacked leaf slice whose length `shape()` requires to be a power of two (`crates/tessera-sumcheck/src/forest.rs`, E0, code read), and every level above the padded leaves is built, bound and streamed like any other.

1. **Fraction trees** ([RFC-0008 'Fraction tree'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0008-logup-and-tables.md#fraction-tree)): an instance of `N` real leaves is padded to `2^n` by lookups of table row 0, leaves `(1, α − t_0)`, with `m_0` raised by `2^n − N`.
2. **Forests** ([RFC-0010 'Tree levels'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0010-proof-schedule.md#tree-levels)): a forest of `R` trees is padded to `2^s` trees with public root values.
3. **Rows inside a forest** ([RFC-0009 'SoftmaxRow'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0009-gadgets.md#softmaxrow) relation 2, [RFC-0010 'Tree levels'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0010-proof-schedule.md#tree-levels)): row `q` has `q + 1` valid keys but a forest's trees share one height `h`, so each row's tree is padded from `q + 1` to `2^h` leaves. The padded leaves are the public constant 1 (the only sound choice; a padded 0 would satisfy relation 2 for every row), excluded from the leaf claim by the public causal selector `C(q, i)` of [RFC-0010 'Relations'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0010-proof-schedule.md#relations).

In every case the padding is a set of identical leaves at public positions. The change, prover-only, in six steps:

1. **Place every padding as aligned blocks.** A padded region `[N, 2^n)` of a level table decomposes into at most `n` aligned blocks `B(p, k) = {x : x_{k+1..n} = p}` (the high `n − k` bits fixed to `p`, the low `k` bits free), one per set bit of `2^n − N`; a row's padding `[q + 1, 2^h)` decomposes the same way inside its tree; a forest's padded trees are one block over the tree index. The decomposition is the bit recursion RFC-0008 'Structured evaluation' already uses for `LT(ρ, a) = Σ_{h<a} eq(ρ, h)`.
2. **Constant per level.** A perfect binary subtree of `2^m` equal leaves has equal nodes at every level: `κ_m = 1` for product trees of ones; `κ_m = (2^m · n · d^{2^m − 1}, d^{2^m})` for fraction leaves `(n, d) = (1, α − t_0)`, so `d^{2^m}` by repeated squaring. Computed once per instance per level, `O(h)` field operations in all.
3. **Never materialize the blocks.** Only the real leaves and the real part of each level are built (`build_levels`), bound and, when streamed, regenerated. A block of `2^k` entries at level `t` is one scalar `κ` and its prefix `p`.
4. **Closed-form round contribution.** Level `t`'s sumcheck proves `Σ_x eq(ρ, x)·V(0 ‖ x)·V(1 ‖ x)` (product trees) or the Papini–Haböck fraction relation (RFC-0008) over `n` variables, binding the lowest variable first. In round `j ≤ k`, with challenges `r_1 … r_{j−1}` bound and node value `z` for `x_j`, a block whose children are the constant `κ` contributes

   `g(κ) · E_j(z)`, `E_j(z) = eq(z, ρ_j) · Π_{i<j} eq(r_i, ρ_i) · Π_{i>k} eq(p_i, ρ_i)`,

   where `g(κ) = κ²` for a product level and `g(κ) = (κ_n κ_d + κ_n κ_d, κ_d²)` combined with the level's challenge for a fraction level; the free coordinates `j + 1 … k` sum to one each. `E_j` is one `Fp2` multiplication per round after an `O(n)` prefix per block; the degree in `z` is 1, so the three message nodes `p(0), p(2), p(3)` cost three multiplications. After round `k` the block is one entry of the folded tables (value `κ` at the point `p`), which the dense kernel takes over unchanged. The engine's padding-round rule `2^{n_max − n_i − j − 1}·S_i` (`lib.rs` 'Padding') is the special case where the whole instance is constant in the leading variables.
5. **Claims unchanged.** The leaf claims at the end of the level are the same multilinear claims as today: the padded entries are part of the committed value and multiplicity polynomials (constant rows, `m_0` raised) and of the virtual key polynomial (table row 0's key, or `C·k + (1 − C)` for softmax rows); nothing in the claim list, the messages, the labels or the verifier changes.
6. **Make the waste visible.** `CommitmentReport` reports padded against real leaves per table instance and per forest, so the planner and the cost model see the multiplier.

For a streamed leaf level (RFC-0018 'Pass structure') the same rule skips the padded tiles: no regeneration, no fold over them, and `N_level` in `c(S) = ⌈log2(N_level · 32 B / free)⌉` counts real leaves.

## Why it could work

- **The padding multipliers are large where it matters (E0, primary: own arithmetic from RISK-2's per-layer counts, Llama-3.1 dimensions, RFC-0010's forest rule; script in the sweep's scratchpad, reproduced in 'Expected gain').** An instance's leaf count is `per-layer count × layers × tokens`. At 405B (126 layers = 0.984 × 2^7) the power-of-two per-layer counts (norms 2 × 16,384, residual rescales 2 × 16,384, RoPE-Q 16,384, V and RoPE-K 1,024) pad by 1.6%, SiLU (53,248 = 13 × 2^12) by 25%, and a shared RoPE Q+K definition (17 × 2^10) by 91%; the `Exp` instance (8,064 × T(T + 1)) by 1.6%. At 70B (80 layers = 1.25 × 2^6; 64 heads × 80 / 2 = 2,560 = 1.25 × 2^11) every lookup instance pads by 1.6× (SiLU, 28,672 = 7 × 2^12, by 1.83×) and the `Exp` instance and `SoftmaxRow` forest by 1.6×: the v0.5 measurement model does 1.6× the tree work the cost model counts.
- **Row padding inside the `SoftmaxRow` forest is the largest multiplier and is model-independent (E0, primary, own arithmetic).** Causal rows `q = 0 … T − 1` hold `T(T + 1)/2` real scores. Padding each row to `2^⌈log2(q + 1)⌉` and grouping rows by height costs `Σ_q 2^⌈log2(q+1)⌉ / (T(T + 1)/2) = 1.333` at every `T` from 1K to 16K; one forest of height `log2 T` costs `2.000`. RFC-0010 leaves the grouping to the schedule ("product trees of one height that the schedule lists together"), so the product-leaf multiplier at 405B is 1.35–2.03 with the forest padding, and 2.13–3.20 at 70B. The cost model's `A·S` counts real scores only (`cost_model.py`: `scores = scores_per_token_per_ctx × context`).
- **Padded leaves are full leaves today (E0, primary, code read).** `Forest::new(leaves, height)` takes `2^{s+h}` materialized leaves, `build_levels` builds every level over them, and `prove_batch` binds them every round (`forest.rs`, `prover.rs`). The level nodes above fraction padding are `Fp2` pairs whose rounds, bytes and streamed passes are paid like any other (RFC-0008, RFC-0018).
- **The engine's structure already admits the closed form (E0, primary, code read).** `prover.rs` contributes an instance's padding rounds as `2^{n_max − n_i − j − 1}·S_i` at every node without materializing the padded tables, and `a_prover_that_omits_the_padding_scale_is_rejected` shows the verifier checks the result; step 4 is the same identity applied to a partial level.
- **Ragged beats padded in the one E1 measurement the corpus holds (E1, primary, single machine).** [SUR-0007](../surveys/SUR-0007-sumcheck.md) finding 4 records the ragged multi-instance GKR for Poseidon2 (row `[Ign26]`: Intel Core i7-1365U, three samples): padding nine regions to the maximum width costs 3.27× the rows and a ragged embedding by a selector `χ_a(x) = Π_{j ≥ w_a} (1 − x_j)` proves 4.0× faster than padding. That selector is RFC-0010's front padding for whole rounds; this idea is the block form for partial levels.
- **The `eq`-mass of an aligned block is a standard closed form (E0, primary).** RFC-0008 'Structured evaluation' uses `LT(ρ, a)` with its `O(n)` bit-recursive form for the verifier's table evaluation; `E_j` in step 4 is the prover-side instance of the same sum. The product-tree kernel's per-leaf work is about 46 `Fp`-multiplication equivalents ([IDEA-0010](IDEA-0010-shared-point-forest-fusion.md), E0; [EXP-0006](../experiments/EXP-0006-product-leaf-cost/README.md) measures 32.2–32.5 ns per leaf on one Apple M4 Max core, E1), against three `Fp2` multiplications per block per round: for a block of `2^k` leaves the saving is a factor of about `2^k / k`.

## Prior rejection check

Checked: [baseline.md §7](../baseline.md#7-already-rejected) and the Alternatives Considered of RFC-0008, RFC-0009, RFC-0010 and RFC-0018 say nothing about the cost of power-of-two padding. RFC-0010 'Drawbacks' names a different waste ("front-padding wastes rounds for small instances in large stages"), and the engine's analytic padding rounds (`lib.rs` 'Padding') cover the whole rounds of a smaller instance in a larger batch, not a partial level of one instance. RFC-0018 Alternatives Considered 2 (per-tile challenges) is about tile-local points, not padding. [IDEA-0010](IDEA-0010-shared-point-forest-fusion.md) shares the `eq` table between instances and keeps the leaf level in the prime subfield; it treats every leaf, padded or not, and is independent of and composable with this idea. New: the partial-level block, its closed-form `eq`-mass, the observation that the 70B configuration pads every tree by 1.6×, and the observation that causal row padding multiplies the product-leaf work by 1.33–2.0 at every model size.

Spec gap found while checking, not a rejection: RFC-0009 'SoftmaxRow' and RFC-0010 'Tree levels' do not name the constant that fills a row's padded leaves, nor whether `SoftmaxRow` rows are grouped by height or stacked at one height. The constant must be 1 (a prover-chosen or zero padding would satisfy relation 2 for every row); the grouping decides the 1.33× against 2.0× factor above. Both belong in RFC-0009 as one sentence each, and the first is a soundness requirement the `soundness-review` skill should check in the leaf-claim code.

## Kill criterion

One of: (a) an E0 reading of `forest.rs`, `prover.rs` and the RFC-0018 leaf-stage kernel plan shows the engine already skips padded tiles and contributes them in closed form (today `Forest` takes a materialized leaf slice of power-of-two length and `build_levels` builds every level over it, so this is not the case on 2026-10-16); or (b) the actual table definitions, forest grouping and proof-unit shapes of the v0.5 and v1.0 configurations give every instance and forest a padding factor below 5%, which the row-padding arithmetic above excludes unless `SoftmaxRow` rows are proven ragged by some other mechanism.

## Cheapest experiment

- **E0 (`s`, done in this note and to be filed as the experiment's `count.py`):** the table of padding factors per table instance and per `SoftmaxRow` forest for 405B and 70B at 1K, 4K and 16K tokens from RISK-2's counts and RFC-0010's forest rule, under two assumptions about which lookups share a definition and two about row grouping.
- **E1 (`s`, reachable here):** in the harness of [EXP-0006](../experiments/EXP-0006-product-leaf-cost/README.md) on the Apple M4 Max, prove (i) a forest of `1.25 × 2^k` trees padded to `2^{k+1}` (the 70B shape) and (ii) a forest of causal rows of lengths `1 … 2^h` grouped by height (the `SoftmaxRow` shape), each with the materialized padding (today's `prove_forests`) and with the analytic blocks (one `κ` and one prefix per block per level); the same for a fraction-proxy level of EXP-0006's shape whose last 37.5% of entries are the constant padded node. Hypothesis: bit-for-bit equal messages and claims, checked by EXP-0006's `tv-core` driver, and nanoseconds per real leaf within 5% of the unpadded forest's. Success criterion: equality holds and the padded-versus-analytic time ratio is within 10% of the padding factor (1.6 and 1.33). Effort `s`, no RFC. Unknown until an H100 run: whether the GPU kernel's tile shape (RFC-0018 'Kernels') makes the blocks coincide with whole tiles, which decides whether the saving on GPU is the full factor or the factor less partial tiles.

Experiment 2026-10-16: [EXP-0009](../experiments/EXP-0009-analytic-padding-blocks/README.md) (E1, one Apple M4 Max core, pre-registered before any code; status `done: inconclusive`). A ragged forest prover that builds, materializes, binds and folds only the real prefix of every tree's level and adds each padded block's contribution in closed form (`κ²·C_j·eq(ρ_j, z)·TAIL(ρ_rest, P)`, `TAIL` by the bit recursion of RFC-0008 'Structured evaluation'; padded trees as one `TAIL` over the tree coordinates) reproduces today's `prove_forests` bit for bit, round messages, final evaluations, leaf claims and transcript state, on causal rows at one height (`T` = 4096, `f` = 2.00), rows grouped by height (`f` = 1.33), the 70B forest shape (640 of 1,024 trees, `f` = 1.6), both paddings together (`f` = 2.13), two unpadded forests and two 1K sizes, every production proof accepted by the `tv-core` driver; with the constant 1 as the padded leaf, the roots are the real products, and the hand case shows a padding of 0 would make every root 0. The padded-versus-analytic time ratio in the same code is 1.832, 1.259, 1.596 and 1.978, that is 0.92–1.00 of `f` (78–99% of the padding's cost recovered), inside the pre-registered ±10%; against today's production kernels, 11–14% faster per leaf than the harness's loops, the candidate is 1.58×, 1.08×, 1.42× and 1.66× faster. The hypothesis's second number failed: the candidate's cost per real leaf exceeds an unpadded forest's by 12.6%, 9.5% and 14.4% on the three ragged-row shapes (0.2% where the padding is whole trees) against the pre-registered 5%, a per-tree constant of about 100 ns per tree per round (short loops, boundary pairs, odd-tail folds) amortized over each row's real entries, 19% at `T` = 1K and an extrapolated 4% at 16K. The cost model with the measured ratios gives −5.3% / −14.2% / −27.3% of μ(16K) for the three scenarios above (from −6.4% / −16.4% / −28.7%). Status `inconclusive`, confidence 0.6 → 0.7, priority 3.8 → 3.7 (9.9 at one height, 19 at 70B). The shared-`eq` variant of both arms (IDEA-0010's mechanism 1) saves a further 22% of the dense leaf cost and gives the same ratios; combined, the analytic prover with shared `eq` is 2.05× / 1.38× / 1.83× / 2.11× faster than today's prover.
