## Mechanism

A second live parameter set, `live-64-fast`, beside `live-64` (RFC-0014 'Parameter sets'); not a default, and not offered in the non-interactive mode, whose proof is archived and keeps rate 1/2. Nothing in the transcript schedule changes.

1. **Code.** `k = 2^22`, `n = 3·2^21 = 6,291,456`. Goldilocks has 3-smooth roots of unity: `p − 1 = 2^32 · 3 · 5 · 17 · 257 · 65537` (E0, factored and checked this session), so a subgroup `L` of order `3·2^21` exists. The codeword lives on the coset `7·L`: 7 generates the whole multiplicative group (E0, checked: `7^((p−1)/q) ≠ 1` for every prime `q | p − 1`), so `7·L` meets the evaluation subgroup `H` of order `2^22` nowhere (an element common to both would put 7 inside the subgroup of order `3·2^22`). Effective rate `ρ' = (k + P_A)/n = 0.6667` to four digits for every `P_A` between 160 and 224 (E0, arithmetic).
2. **Encoder.** In evaluation form, as RFC-0011 'Encoding' does today: an inverse NTT of size `k` (11 butterflies per committed element, unchanged), then a mixed-radix coset NTT of size `3·2^21`: 21 radix-2 levels over `n` points cost `21 · (n/k) / 2 = 15.75` butterflies per committed element, and one radix-3 stage priced at 2.5 radix-2 equivalents per 3 points costs `2.5 · n/(3k) = 1.25`: 17.0 butterfly-equivalents per committed element against 23.0 at rate 1/2 (E0, hand count; the radix-3 price is an estimate, see kill criterion (c)). Total 11 + 17 = 28 against the 34 of [EXP-0002](../experiments/EXP-0002-commit-cost-calibration/README.md) (E0). With [IDEA-0002](IDEA-0002-coefficient-form-rows.md) composed (coefficient-form rows, no inverse NTT) only the 17 remain, against 23.
3. **Hash.** 1.5 symbols per committed element, `B = 32` rows per block unchanged. A column step still hashes 32 symbols plus the 32-byte chain value (288 bytes, 5 compressions, as EXP-0002 counts) but now covers `32 · 2/3 = 21.3` committed elements instead of 16: 0.234 compressions per committed element against 0.3125 (E0). Under IDEA-0014's keyed chain (no chain value in the input), 4 compressions per step: 0.1875 against 0.25 (E0). Hashed codeword bytes per committed element 16 → 12.
4. **Proximity.** `τ_A` from the Johnson-regime calculator of RFC-0021 at `η = 0.003`: 223 columns live (64.0 query bits; field terms 69.6 bits under [BCHKS25] and 82.3 bits under [ArkLib26], the same as `live-64`'s at rate 1/2 to the first decimal because the field term depends on `η` and `n`, not on the rate). Unique decoding would need 244 (E0, `proximity.py`). About 279 columns would be needed at 80 one-shot bits; no non-interactive variant is proposed.
5. **Zero knowledge.** `P_A` raised from 160 to at least 224 so that `P_A ≥ τ_A` holds with margin (RFC-0011 'Encoding' requires fewer opened columns than mask coefficients); `ρ'` is unchanged to four digits and `k + P_A ≤ n` holds trivially.
6. **Descriptor.** `AdviceLayoutParams.log2_inv_rate: u8` (RFC-0011) cannot express 2/3. The layout parameters gain either a rate as numerator/denominator or `log2_n` plus a 3-smooth flag, encoded canonically (RFC-0003) and bound into `commitment_id` as today (RFC-0011 'Column digests and root'); the decoder rejects any value that is not one of the two parameter sets' with a typed error (INV-TCE-2). Tier A.
7. **Verifier.** `tv-field` gains a mixed-radix `Fp2` coset NTT of size `3·2^21` for `enc(u_g)` of the `G + 1` combined rows (RFC-0011 'Sizes', verifier-work row), written from the specification (SP-6), with a 3-smooth root constant and `spec/vectors/` cases from the Python oracle; `tv-core`'s proximity check takes `τ_A = 223` from the parameter set.

## Why it could work

- E0, primary (this session, over [EXP-0002](../experiments/EXP-0002-commit-cost-calibration/README.md)'s per-element operation table): scaling the NTT ops by `28/34` and the hash ops by `0.234/0.3125` gives total int32 ops 969 → 780 at the low end (`721 · 28/34 + 248 · 0.75 = 594 + 186`) and 1,574 → 1,274 at the high end (`1,274 · 28/34 + 300 · 0.75 = 1,049 + 225`): −19.5% and −19.0%. On the `counted` profile's `c_c = 94` ps (E0 over E1 unit costs) that is `c_c ≈ 76` ps. With IDEA-0002 composed the coset-only encoder falls 23 → 17 (−26%) and `c_c` about −23%, roughly 72 ps (E0).
- E0, primary (`python3 -I research/tools/proximity.py --log2-k 22 --rate 2/3 --mask 160 --bits 64 --bchks25 --arklib`, this session): unique decoding gives `τ_A = 244` (0.263 bits per query, 1.132 GB opened); the Johnson regime gives 223 at `η = 0.003` with 64.0 query bits, admissible under both [BCHKS25] and [ArkLib26], opened columns 1.035 GB against 0.603 GB at rate 1/2 and `τ_A = 130` (+0.43 GB, +72%). The Johnson gain over unique decoding shrinks from 16% at rate 1/2 to 9% at rate 2/3, because the radius `1 − √ρ` approaches `(1 − ρ)/2` as `ρ` grows: RFC-0021 helps this idea less than it helped the baseline.
- E0 over E1 unit costs ([SUR-0012](../surveys/SUR-0012-verifier.md) synthesis 1 and its budget row, one M4 Max core): verifier column hashing 0.61 → 1.05 s and the `(G + 1) · τ_A` inner products 2.2 → 3.8 s (both `× 223/130`), the 13 s one-core budget → about 15 s; the `G + 1` coset NTTs of the combined rows get 25% shorter. Inside SC-3's 300 s by a wide margin, and SUR-0012's parallel verifier scales it the same way.
- E0, primary ([E0-hash], [SUR-0006](../surveys/SUR-0006-hash.md) synthesis 3): at a fixed rate the chain hashes `8/ρ` bytes per committed element plus the chain value; the floor at rate 1/2 is 0.25 compressions per element, so hashed bytes move only with rate. The hash is 19–26% of `c_c` in EXP-0002's counts and 37.5% of the CPU time (E1, EXP-0002); as the encoder ideas land its share rises.
- E0, secondary ([Uulong26], E1 on an RX 7900 XTX; [E0-SUR5] translation; [SUR-0005](../surveys/SUR-0005-commitments.md) synthesis 1): the Goldilocks GPU NTT is memory-bound from 2^25 points at 540–560 GB/s effective, so a 25% shorter transform moves 25% fewer bytes through HBM and the gain survives on a bandwidth-bound kernel too (the only public Goldilocks GPU NTT figure, another GPU).

## Prior rejection check

- RFC-0014 'Parameter sets' and RFC-0011 'Encoding' fix rate 1/2 (`ρ ≤ 0.51`) for the advice without an Alternatives Considered entry on the advice rate; the source (§7) chose rate 1/2 as "bandwidth bound" encoding. RISK-8 states the hash budget at rate 1/2 (about 530 GB/s per H100); rate 2/3 lowers it by a quarter.
- [IDEA-0013](IDEA-0013-advice-rate-quarter.md) / [EXP-0004](../experiments/EXP-0004-advice-rate-quarter/README.md) refuted the opposite move (rate 1/4) on `Δμ` (+10.6% to +21.2%) and kept it as a size knob with the reopening condition "`c_c` well below 30 ps"; the present idea is its mirror and takes the same shape (a parameter set, not a default).
- [SUR-0006](../surveys/SUR-0006-hash.md) candidate ideas priced the mirror at `c_c` −15% to −20% and `Δμ` −3.1% to −4.2% at 16K under unique decoding (`τ_A ≈ 245`, 1.14 GB opened, verifier hashing +58%) and judged it out of direction because proof size and verification time are the scoreboard's structural deficits.
- [baseline.md §7](../baseline.md#7-already-rejected) has no row on the advice rate.

New now: (1) the Johnson-regime numbers (223 columns, not 245; 1.035 GB, not 1.14 GB), with the field term shown to be rate-independent to the first decimal; (2) the `counted` profile puts `c_c` at 30% of `μ` against 21% under the corrected profile, so the same relative cut on `c_c` is worth −5.7% rather than −4.2% of `μ`; (3) [SUR-0011](../surveys/SUR-0011-field.md) and SUR-0006 synthesis 3 locate half of `c_c`'s field dependence and the whole hash floor in the symbol bytes, which only rate moves; (4) [SUR-0012](../surveys/SUR-0012-verifier.md) synthesis 1 finds verification a parallel 13 s inside a 300 s budget, so +72% on the column work is not a gate; (5) the live-only framing: the proof is streamed once and never stored, so +0.43 GB costs transfer time (3.4 s at 1 Gbit/s, 0.03 s at 100 Gbit/s; E0 arithmetic), not storage.

Checked and closed this round under the same lens, so that nobody reopens them: small-value commitments over Goldilocks. Packing two `b`-bit values into one `Fp` element (or into one `Fp2` symbol) is unsound here because every consumer (the LogUp fraction leaves, the matmul sumcheck) ends in one evaluation claim per packed polynomial at the same point while the commitment yields one linear equation, so a prover solves for the free component at the last round; each new evaluation point adds two unknowns and at most two equations. [SUR-0005](../surveys/SUR-0005-commitments.md) synthesis 5 reached the same conclusion from the subfield structure (`Fp ⊂ Fp2` is the only step; [DP26] needs a tower). No idea. Also checked: the weight commitment is already at rate 1/4 (RFC-0012) and the weight term is 0.02 of `μ`, so no weight-rate idea.

## Kill criterion

Any one of: (a) the maintainer's scoreboard priority: +0.43 GB on the opened-columns term, which a Ligerito level 2 ([SUR-0005](../surveys/SUR-0005-commitments.md) synthesis 8) would make the whole proof, is judged not worth −6% of `μ`; (b) the RFC-0020 v0.1 gate measures `c_c ≤ 40` ps, where the same −19% is worth under −3% of `μ` (cost model, E0); (c) the radix-3 stage costs more than 2.5 radix-2 equivalents per 3 points on the GPU (or the mixed-radix kernel loses the memory-bound plateau of [Uulong26]), or the mixed-radix `Fp2` verifier NTT plus the tier-A descriptor change are judged dearer than a −6% knob; (d) `P_A ≥ 224` or the re-evaluated list bound `L` and claim-binding term push the field term below RFC-0021's 82-bit floor under [ArkLib26] at `η = 0.003`.

## Cheapest experiment

- **Hypothesis.** In the EXP-0002 harness on the M4 Max, a mixed-radix `coset_ntt` of size `3·2^21` plus `encode_row` at rate 2/3 and `absorb_block` at 1.5 symbols per committed element cost `0.81 ± 0.03` of the rate-1/2 encoder-and-chain time per committed element (prediction −19%, E0), and the re-evaluated `L` and claim-binding term keep the field term above 82 bits.
- **Method.** E0 first (hours, `s`): run EXP-0005's `terms.py` at `n = 3·2^21`, `D = 2^22 + 223`, `P_A = 224`, and `proximity.py` with `--mask 224` to confirm `τ_A = 223` and the field terms; count the radix-3 stage exactly in `count.py`. Then E1 (days, `s` to `m`): add a radix-3 stage to `NttPlan` in the EXP-0002 harness (its own `[workspace]`, SPDX headers), the 3-smooth root of order `3·2^21`, `encode_row` on the coset `7·L` with `P = 224`, and `absorb_block` over `3·2^21` columns with `B = 32`; same machine, same inputs, same harness as the rate-1/2 baseline (README rule 4); report the ratio and the fit `F`.
- **Evidence reachable here.** E0 (counts, proximity, extractor terms) and E1 (M4 Max). Effort `s` for the experiment; `l` for the idea as a whole (tier-A descriptor and parameter-set RFC, a new verifier NTT written independently, `spec/vectors/` for the 3-smooth transform).
- **Unknown until an H100 run (E2).** Whether the mixed-radix kernel keeps the memory-bound plateau and whether the radix-3 price holds on CUDA; E2 only confirms the ratio. The idea's decision is the scoreboard trade (`μ` −6% against +0.43 GB and +72% of the verifier's column work), not a measurement.
