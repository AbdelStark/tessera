## Mechanism

**Finding** (E0, primary: the three RFC texts, read 2026-10-16). The opening pass P4 regenerates every non-zero row to fold `u_g = Σ_row c_g(row) · A_row` (RFC-0011 step 4). The column indices are drawn only after `u_g` is absorbed (RFC-0014 orders 8 and 10), and necessarily so: indices known before `u_g` would let a cheating prover fit `u_g` to the `τ_A + 1` linear constraints the column check imposes. The answer to each distinct index is every non-zero row's codeword symbol at that column (step 7), and the codeword is not stored (RFC-0011 Alternatives Considered 1: terabytes; RFC-0018 Alternative 1: no NVMe). So `answer_columns` must regenerate every non-zero row a third time and evaluate its masked polynomial `g = f + Z_H · m` at the `τ_A` distinct queried positions: a fifth pass (1× forward, RISK-9) plus per-element encode work. Neither appears in RFC-0018's pass table, in `n_regen = 4`, or in the cost model, where `c_c` is paid once per committed element. RFC-0011 'Drawbacks' ("every opening needs a full regeneration pass") and the source's §7 ("the opening recomputes rows in a second streaming pass") both count one opening-time pass where the transcript order needs two.

**Change**, two parts, both prover-only.

1. **Pricing.** Add the row `P5 column answers | after order 10 | every committed row | 1× forward` to RFC-0018's pass table; `regen_full_equivalents` 4 → 5 in `tools/cost_model.py` and `n_regen` 4 → 5 in 08 §1; a per-element encode-only term for P5 in the model (`c_c` today bundles encode and hash; P5 encodes and does not hash). Issue to the RFC-0018 and RFC-0020 owners. Consequence for SUR-0003's "P4 → 0" seed (ledger item 24): if the combined rows are folded from stage data, P5 becomes the only opening-time regeneration, so the floor for `n_regen` is P1 + P3 + P5, not P1 + P3.

2. **Pruned evaluation.** In P5, per regenerated row, compute only the `τ_A ≤ 163` needed symbols by output pruning of the Gentleman–Sande (decimation-in-frequency) coset NTT:
   - Form the coefficient buffer of size `n = 2^23` exactly as the P1 encoder does (coefficient form, IDEA-0002: `f_i · 7^i` at `i < k`, `m_i · 7^{k+i}` at `k + i`; today's evaluation form: the size-`k` interpolation first, then the spread and the mask).
   - Run `s = ⌈log2 τ_distinct⌉ = 8` full DIF levels over the buffer (`n/2` butterflies each). After `s` DIF levels the transform splits into `2^s = 256` independent sub-transforms of size `n / 2^s = 2^15`, the `r`-th of which produces exactly the outputs whose index is congruent to `r` modulo `2^s` (in the DIF output order; with the bit-reversed output of a natural-order DIF this is the top `s` bits of the output index, a fixed public map either way).
   - Continue only the sub-transforms that contain a queried position, and compute each needed output as one direct evaluation of its sub-transform: `2^15` multiply-adds with precomputed twiddles. For uniform draws of `τ ≤ 163` distinct indices over `256` residues, most sub-transforms hold zero or one queried position, so direct evaluation beats finishing the sub-transform (`15 · 2^14` butterflies).
   - The mask's `τ` values `Z_H(x_j) · m(x_j)` (or `x_j^k · m(x_j)` in coefficient form) are folded into the buffer before the levels, as in P1, so no separate evaluation is needed; if the buffer excludes the mask, they cost `P · τ ≈ 200 × 163 = 3.3×10^4` multiply-adds per row, negligible against `2^22` elements.
   - Tiling as P1 (`B` rows per block); the `s` full levels run in IDEA-0003's L2-resident tile layout (the first 8 DIF levels of a `2^23`-point transform touch strides `2^22 … 2^15`, so they are the HBM-bound part; the sub-transform tails are register- or shared-memory-resident).

   Operation count per committed element (`k = 2^22`, E0, primary, own count 2026-10-16, the standard output-pruned DIF accounting of [SB93]): `8` butterflies for the full levels + `163 × 2^15 / 2^22 ≈ 1.3` multiply-adds for the tails ≈ `9.3` butterfly-equivalents, against `23` for a full coset NTT of size `n` in coefficient form (IDEA-0002) or `34` today (`11` for the size-`k` interpolation + `23`). If rows stay in evaluation form, the size-`k` interpolation stays: pruned `20.3` against `34`.

   The queried indices are public (RFC-0014 order 10, the verifier's draw), so the pruning pattern leaks nothing. Bit-exact equality with the P1 encoder's symbols is required and is what RFC-0011's own self-check already demands ([RFC-0011 'Failure modes'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0011-advice-commitment.md#failure-modes): "the prover recomputes one random column's leaf before sending", `ERR-INTERNAL` on mismatch). The leaves and the Merkle tree (256 MiB) are kept from P1, so P5 hashes nothing; the multiproof is read off the stored tree.

## Why it could work

- **The pass is forced by the protocol order, not by an implementation choice** (E0, primary, from the RFC texts). Ligero-family provers answer column queries from the encoded matrix they keep: [NA25] Ligerito reports prover memory about 2× the largest encoded matrix (E1, primary; [SUR-0005](../surveys/SUR-0005-commitments.md) notes this is why Tessera's level 1 must stream), [WHV24] Ligetron keeps an `O(√|C|)` encoded buffer (E1, primary). Tessera's encoded advice is `2.4×10^12 × 16 B ≈ 38 TB` per 16K-token unit (E0, [RFC-0011 'Sizes'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0011-advice-commitment.md#sizes-at-the-default-parameters-16k-tokens-16k-context-405b): 2.4×10^12 committed elements at rate 1/2, 8 B per symbol) and about 2.4 TB for a 1K-token unit, against 0.2 TB of free HBM and 1 TiB of host RAM (RFC-0018, E0). SUR-0003 synthesis 1 confirms RFC-0018 Alternative 1 with numbers (340 s of NVMe reads against 12 s of regeneration, E0). Regeneration is the only route, so the pass exists whether or not it is priced.
- **Cost of the omission** (E0, primary, [tools/cost_model.py](../tools/cost_model.py), run 2026-10-16). Under the `counted` profile (`c_c = 94` ps, of which the encoder is 74–81% by int32 operation count, [EXP-0002](../experiments/EXP-0002-commit-cost-calibration/README.md) Table "Quantity", E0 counts fitted to E1), one more regeneration (`regen` 4 → 5) and a full re-encode (+73 ps per committed element) take `μ(1K)` from 17.3 to 22.8 (+31.4%) and `μ(16K)` from 78.8 to 98.0 (+24.3%). Under `corrected` (`c_c = 30` ps, encoder +23 ps), `μ(1K)` 8.9 → 11.3 (+27.1%) and `μ(16K)` 35.1 → 41.9 (+19.1%), which leaves the SC-2 ceilings (12 and 45, [00 §7](../../docs/spec/00-overview.md#7-success-criteria)) standing by 6% and 7% only. The honest baseline is the one the roadmap should be planning against.
- **Pruning removes 40–60% of P5's encode work with no protocol change** (E0, primary, own count above): 9.3 butterfly-equivalents per element against 23 in coefficient form (−60%), 20.3 against 34 in today's evaluation form (−40%). On EXP-0002's CPU calibration (0.899 ns per radix-2 butterfly, E1, Apple M4 Max one core) a full `encode_row` costs 32.1 ns per committed element and the pruned coefficient-form P5 would cost about `9.3 × 0.899 ≈ 8.4` ns, if the eight full levels keep the in-cache butterfly cost; EXP-0002 found the out-of-cache levels of a `2^23`-point transform "cost almost nothing extra on this machine", but on a GPU those eight levels are exactly the HBM-touching ones (see Kill criterion).
- **The regeneration itself is the floor.** One native forward on tensor cores per P5 is the part no kernel removes; only storing the codeword would, and that is rejected with numbers (SUR-0003 synthesis 1). The idea composes with IDEA-0002 (coefficient form removes the size-`k` interpolation from P5 as well as from P1) and with IDEA-0003's tile layout; it is neutral to the keyed-chain, `B = 28` and radix ideas (P5 hashes nothing).

## Security gate

| Property | Effect | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| Soundness bits (G-4, RFC-0014) | No message, label, challenge order or verifier check changes; RFC-0014 rows 5 and 7–10 stay as they are. The symbols sent are the committed ones (bit-exact with P1 by construction of the pruned transform, which computes the same polynomial's values at the same points); a mismatch between P5 and P1 is a completeness failure caught by RFC-0011's self-check (`ERR-INTERNAL` before sending), never the acceptance of a false proof. The query order the finding rests on (indices after the combined rows) must stay: drawing indices before `u_g` is unsound, and no re-ordering is proposed. The pricing part changes estimates, not the protocol. | pass |
| Uniqueness upstream of the logits (SP-3, RFC-0009 Lemma 0) | Prover-runtime only; no gadget, table, range or committed value changes. | pass |
| Zero knowledge (G-5, RFC-0013) | The opened symbols are already revealed by the protocol; P5 reveals exactly those. The pruning pattern (which sub-transforms are continued) depends only on the public indices. The mask coefficients are regenerated from the per-proof mask stream exactly as in P1 ([RFC-0011 'Streaming procedure'](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0011-advice-commitment.md#streaming-procedure-witness-pass)), so no mask is reused across proofs or drawn afresh; rows and masks stay in `Secret<T>`. P5's timing in the run manifest is public, as every pass time already is (RFC-0018 'Pass structure'). | pass |
| Verifier independence (SP-6) | Verifier untouched; nothing under `verifier/` changes. | pass |
| Post-quantum (hash-based commitments) | Unchanged: the same BLAKE3 column chains and Merkle tree, read from P1's stored tree. | pass |

Fiat–Shamir ([RFC-0004](../../docs/rfcs/RFC-0004-hashing-and-transcript.md), INV-FS-1..4): no new message, no new challenge. Redaction ([05 §3](../../docs/spec/05-observability.md#3-data-classification-and-redaction)): P5 logs its time and the public indices, nothing secret-class. Robustness (SP-8): prover side only.

- [SUR-0003](../surveys/SUR-0003-passes.md) synthesis 1 ("a Ligero-family commitment costs exactly two full passes") and synthesis 2 ("the floor for `n_regen` is about 2 full-forward equivalents") count commit and combined rows and never the column answers; the ledger's item 24 (P4 → 0 from stage folds) and SUR-0003's candidate "Host-RAM advice cache for small units" likewise address P4. SUR-0003's second candidate would, if adopted for a 1K-token unit, also serve P5 from the cached advice (not the codeword), but the cache is sized for linear-layer advice only and does not fit attention advice.
- [baseline.md §7](../baseline.md#7-already-rejected) has no row on pass counting or on pruned evaluation (read 2026-10-16). RFC-0018 Alternative 2 (per-tile challenges) and 3 (multi-variable rounds) are unrelated.
- None covers the idea, after the check. What is new: the gap itself (E0, from the three RFC texts side by side) and a pruned-evaluation kernel that cuts P5's encode work by 40–60% without any protocol change.

## Kill criterion

For the finding: a reading of RFC-0011, RFC-0014 or RFC-0018 that produces every row's queried symbols from data a priced pass already keeps within the memory budget (none found: the backend interface's separate `answer_columns`, the order of RFC-0014's rows 8 and 10 and the rejected codeword storage exclude it). The RFC-0018 owner confirming that P4's "1× forward" was meant to cover both opening-time regenerations would move the finding from "omitted" to "under-counted by one pass and the encode work", not kill it.

For the kernel: on the M4 Max, the pruned evaluation at `τ = 130` and `163` uniform distinct positions is not at least 1.5× faster per row than a full coset NTT of size `2^23` plus a gather (the eight full levels being memory-bound at 64 MB per row buffer), or the direct-evaluation tails dominate because clustered residues modulo `2^s` force many sub-transforms to be finished in full.

## Cheapest experiment

**E0 now** (effort `s`, done in part above): the pass count from the three RFC texts and the cost-model scenarios under both profiles; an issue to the RFC-0018 and RFC-0020 owners to add P5 to the pass table, to 08 §1 and to the cost model (with a separate encode-only term, since P5 does not hash).

**E1 here** (effort `s`). Hypothesis, pre-registered here: in the same harness on one M4 Max performance core, `prune_eval(coefficients, mask, queries)` (eight full Gentleman–Sande levels plus direct evaluation of the needed outputs) is at least 2× faster per row than a full coset NTT of size `2^23` plus gather in coefficient form, and at least 1.4× faster in today's evaluation form (with the size-`k` interpolation in both arms), at `k = 2^22`, `P = 200`, `τ ∈ {130, 163}` uniform distinct positions; and its `τ` symbols equal those gathered from `encode_row`'s codeword bit for bit. Method: a scratch Cargo workspace (empty `[workspace]` table) depending on `tessera-pcs` and `tessera-field` by path, reusing `gentleman_sande` and the twiddle tables of `crates/tessera-pcs/src/ntt.rs`; RFC-0020 protocol (3 warm-ups, 10 measured, median and coefficient of variation) as in EXP-0002; equality checked at the crate's documented small case (`crates/tessera-pcs/src/lib.rs`, `k = 8`) and at full size; the ratio to a full coset NTT and the implied P5 encode cost per element reported as an E0 translation with EXP-0002's `count.py` figures. Evidence level reachable here: E1. What stays unknown until an H100 run: whether the eight full levels are HBM-bound on the GPU (the fused-tile question of IDEA-0003, E2 at the RFC-0020 v0.1 gate) and the native cost of the regeneration itself (E2/E3: v0.3 pass counter, v0.5 measurement).

**Run 2026-10-16 as [EXP-0007](../experiments/EXP-0007-pruned-column-answer-pass/README.md), `done: validated`** (E1, primary, one M4 Max performance core, RFC-0020 protocol, every symbol bit-exact with the production encoder): the output-pruned DIF is 3.32× / 3.19× faster per row than the full transform in coefficient form at `τ = 130 / 163` (99.8 → 30.1 / 31.3 ms per row, 23.8 → 7.2 / 7.5 ns per committed element) and 1.74× / 1.73× in evaluation form (139.6 → 80.1 / 80.9 ms), against the pre-registered 2× and 1.4×; the kill criterion (1.5×) is missed by more than 2×. The exact count is 8.76 operations per committed element at `τ = 163` with the per-branch rule (skip, one-sided descent, split), against 9.27 for the fixed eight levels above (the 9.3 of the Mechanism section, confirmed) and 23 for the full transform (E0). A live verifier cannot slow the pass by its indices: one residue class modulo 256 runs at 11.4× and pairs `(j, j + n/2)` at 3.55×. Cost model (`counted`, `regen` 5): μ(16K) 92.7 → 83.8 (−9.6%) in coefficient form and 98.0 → 90.2 (−8.0%) in evaluation form against the honest baselines of the same form. What stays E2: whether the eight large-stride levels are HBM-bound on the H100, which bounds the GPU ratio between 1.1× and 2.9× (E0).
