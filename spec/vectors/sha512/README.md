# SHA-512 test vectors

The three files here are NIST's byte-oriented SHA-512 test vectors from the Cryptographic Algorithm Validation Program (CAVP), copied unmodified from `shabytetestvectors.zip`. That archive was downloaded from [NIST CSRC](https://csrc.nist.gov/CSRC/media/Projects/Cryptographic-Algorithm-Validation-Program/documents/shs/shabytetestvectors.zip) on 2026-10-24 and its SHA-256 was `929ef80b7b3418aca026643f6f248815913b60e01741a44bba9e118067f4c9b8`, the archive [`../sha256/`](../sha256/README.md) was copied from. The message files were generated by CAVS 11.0 and the Monte Carlo file by CAVS 11.1, in 2011. As works of the United States government they are in the public domain.

| File | SHA-256 | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `SHA512ShortMsg.rsp` | `e53a36c03609e5a3e3cc4b6e117a499db7864c23ec825c6cec99503a45f40764` | 129 messages of 0 to 128 bytes, each with its digest |
| `SHA512LongMsg.rsp` | `b1f3f05d5c209777954d49521d7ea1349447c36a0c52849e044bc397a27dd410` | 128 messages of 227 to 12,800 bytes, each with its digest |
| `SHA512Monte.rsp` | `8ca78659286c2f01667a98fc7accd32fc171ae7b24ac00f1a8ce6b77770247fa` | the Monte Carlo test: a seed, and the digest after each of 100 rounds of 1,000 chained hashes |

In each file, `Len` is the message length in bits, `Msg` the message in hex (`00` for the empty message), and `MD` the digest. The Monte Carlo procedure is the pseudocode of Figure 1 in NIST's [SHA Validation System](https://csrc.nist.gov/CSRC/media/Projects/Cryptographic-Algorithm-Validation-Program/documents/shs/SHAVS.pdf): `MD0 = MD1 = MD2 = Seed`; for `i` from 3 to 1002, `MDi = SHA-512(MD(i−3) ‖ MD(i−2) ‖ MD(i−1))`; `MD1002` is the round's `MD` and the next round's seed.

The verifier's SHA-512 in `verifier/crates/tv-hash` checks against every case of all three files. That SHA-512 is the hash of the verifier's Ed25519, which checks recorder manifests' signatures (RFC-0060 'Signatures').
