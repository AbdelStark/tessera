# SHA-256 test vectors

The three files here are NIST's byte-oriented SHA-256 test vectors from the Cryptographic Algorithm Validation Program (CAVP), copied unmodified from `shabytetestvectors.zip`. That archive was downloaded from [NIST CSRC](https://csrc.nist.gov/CSRC/media/Projects/Cryptographic-Algorithm-Validation-Program/documents/shs/shabytetestvectors.zip) and its SHA-256 was `929ef80b7b3418aca026643f6f248815913b60e01741a44bba9e118067f4c9b8`. The files were generated by CAVS 11.0 in 2011. As works of the United States government they are in the public domain.

| File | SHA-256 | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `SHA256ShortMsg.rsp` | `75e1cb83994638481808e225b9eb0c1ebd0c232d952ac42b61abce6363be283c` | 65 messages of 0 to 64 bytes, each with its digest |
| `SHA256LongMsg.rsp` | `6fac36f37360bcf74ffcf4465c18e30d6d5a04cc90885b901fc3130c16060974` | 64 messages of 163 to 6,400 bytes, each with its digest |
| `SHA256Monte.rsp` | `29ea30c6bb4b84e425fb8c1d731c6bb852dac935825f2bd1143e5d3c4f10bfb9` | the Monte Carlo test: a seed, and the digest after each of 100 rounds of 1,000 chained hashes |

In each file, `Len` is the message length in bits, `Msg` the message in hex (`00` for the empty message), and `MD` the digest. The Monte Carlo procedure is the pseudocode of Figure 1 in NIST's [SHA Validation System](https://csrc.nist.gov/CSRC/media/Projects/Cryptographic-Algorithm-Validation-Program/documents/shs/SHAVS.pdf): `MD0 = MD1 = MD2 = Seed`; for `i` from 3 to 1002, `MDi = SHA-256(MD(i−3) ‖ MD(i−2) ‖ MD(i−1))`; `MD1002` is the round's `MD` and the next round's seed.

The verifier's SHA-256 in `verifier/crates/tv-hash` checks against every case of all three files. That SHA-256 hashes the recorder logs of RFC-0015.
